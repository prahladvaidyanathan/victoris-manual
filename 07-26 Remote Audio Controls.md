# Remote Audio Controls
Basic audio functions can be controlled using the switches on the steering wheel and instrument panel. These controls are available when the ignition is in ACC or ON mode.

For detailed operation of the audio or navigation system, refer to the infotainment system manual provided with the vehicle.
## Controls
**Volume Switch:** Press the "+" side to increase volume and the "–" side to decrease it. Volume continues to change until the switch is released. You can also turn the volume knob on the instrument panel clockwise to increase and counter-clockwise to decrease.

**Mute:** Press the volume knob to mute; press it again to unmute.

**Select Switch:** Press to cycle through broadcast stations or tracks according to the currently selected mode.

**Hands-free Switch:** Used to activate hands-free calling functionality. Requires prior setup — refer to the audio manual for details.

**Voice Recognition Switch:** Used to activate voice recognition functionality. Requires prior setup — refer to the audio manual for details.
