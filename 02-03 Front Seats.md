# Front Seats
## Seat Adjustment

> **WARNING:**
> - Never attempt to adjust the driver's seat or seatback while driving. The seat or seatback could move unexpectedly, causing loss of control. Check that the driver's seat and seatback are properly adjusted before driving.
> - To avoid excessive seat belt slack, check that the seats are adjusted before the seat belts are fastened.
> - All seatbacks should always be in an upright position when driving, or seat belt effectiveness may be reduced. Seat belts are designed to offer maximum protection when seatbacks are in the upright position.
> - If you recline the seatback more than necessary, the seat belt and SRS airbag system may not function properly in the event of an accident, resulting in serious injury. When driving, do not recline the seatback more than necessary.
> - Do not place a cushion between the seatback and your back. You may not be able to drive in a correct posture, and the seat belt and head restraint may not provide their best performance, possibly causing severe injury in an accident.
> - Do not place any object under the front seat. If an object gets stuck under the seat, the seat may not latch properly and may cause injury. An object caught under the seat may also cause the following: the seat will not latch; parts under the seat may be damaged; a cigarette lighter or spray can on the floor may ignite accidentally.

> **CAUTION:** When adjusting the seat, observe the following:
> - Your hands or feet may get caught and be injured. Do not put your hands or feet under the seat or near the moving parts.
> - Passengers may be injured by a moving seat. Check your surroundings and ensure the safety of passengers.
## Manual Seat (if equipped)

> **WARNING:** If the seat is not securely latched, it may move unexpectedly while driving and result in an accident. After adjusting the seat, check it by moving it back and forth to ensure it is securely latched.

> **CAUTION:** If you operate the reclining lever while away from the seatback, the seatback may suddenly spring up, resulting in injury. Do not operate the reclining lever while away from the seatback. When operating it, make sure to support it with your hand.

**Seat position adjustment lever (1):** Pull the lever up and slide the seat.

**Seatback angle adjustment lever (2):** Pull the lever up and move the seatback.

**Seat height adjustment lever (3) (if equipped):** Pull the lever up to raise the seat. Push the lever down to lower the seat. After adjustment, move the seat and seatback back and forth to check that it is securely latched.

> **NOTE:** To operate the seatback smoothly, pull the seatback adjustment lever (2) in the upward direction. Ensure you move the seatback only after the lever is fully up. After adjusting the seatback, release the lever gently and check that the seatback is locked by moving it forward and backward.
## Power Seat (if equipped)

> **NOTICE:**
> - If you adjust the power seat for a long time with the engine off, the battery may discharge.
> - The power seat is driven by electric motors. Stop operating the seat once the adjustment is done. Continuous operation may cause the motors to stop temporarily; they will resume normal operation after some time.

**Seat position adjustment (1) — driver's seat:** Operate the switch and slide the seat.

**Seat height adjustment (2) — driver's seat:** Operate the switch and raise or lower the entire seat.

**Cushion angle adjustment (3) — driver's seat:** Operate the switch and raise or lower the front of the cushion.

**Seatback angle adjustment (4) — driver's seat:** Operate the switch and adjust the angle of the seatback.

> **WARNING:**
> - Do not leave children unattended in the vehicle. Children can be injured if part of their body gets caught by the seat during operation.
> - Before operating the power seat, check that there are no obstacles or belongings under the seat that could get caught during movement. Failure to do so may cause damage to belongings or the seat.
## Head Restraints

Head restraints are designed to help reduce the risk of neck injuries in the case of an accident. Adjust the head restraint so that the centre of the restraint is level with the top of your ears. If this is not possible for very tall passengers, adjust the head restraint as high as possible.

To raise the front head restraint, pull it upward until it clicks. To lower it, push down the head restraint while holding in the lock button (1). To remove it entirely (for cleaning, replacement, etc.), push in the lock button (1) and pull the head restraint all the way out.

> **NOTE:** It may be necessary to recline the seatback to provide enough overhead clearance to remove the head restraint.

> **WARNING:**
> - Never drive with the head restraints removed. Without the head restraint, a passenger's backward tilting cannot be prevented in a collision, and the impact to the head cannot be reduced during sudden braking or a collision. This may cause severe injury.
> - If the head restraint is installed back to front or not installed securely, it may not provide best performance in an emergency, resulting in severe injury. If installed back to front, you cannot adjust the height or install it securely. Always install the head restraint securely in the correct direction.
> - Do not attempt to adjust the head restraint while driving. Being distracted by head restraint adjustment could lead to an accident.
