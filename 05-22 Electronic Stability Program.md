# Electronic Stability Program (ESP®)
ESP® is a registered trademark of Mercedes-Benz Group AG.

The Electronic Stability Program (ESP®) helps control the vehicle during cornering if the front or rear wheels skid. It also assists in maintaining traction when accelerating on loose or slippery surfaces by regulating engine output and selectively applying the brakes.

The ESP® incorporates the following systems:

**Anti-lock Brake System (ABS)** — refer to [[05-23 Brakes]].

**Brake Assist System** — refer to [[05-23 Brakes]].

**Traction Control System (TCS):** Automatically helps prevent wheel spin when starting or accelerating on slippery surfaces. When wheel spin is detected, the system applies the front or rear brakes and reduces engine power to limit spin.

**Stability Control System:** Provides integrated control of ABS, traction control, and engine management to help prevent skidding when cornering on slippery surfaces or when the steering wheel is turned abruptly.

> **WARNING:** ESP® cannot enhance driving stability in all situations and does not control the entire braking system. It cannot prevent accidents caused by excessive speed in turns or hydroplaning. Only safe and attentive driving can prevent accidents. Never use the capabilities of an ESP®-equipped vehicle as a substitute for careful driving.

## ESP® Warning Light
When there is a problem with the ESP® system with the ignition in ON mode, this light comes on. While it is on:
- The traction control system and stability control system will not work
- The hill hold control system may not work
- ABS will still work
- Brake assist may not work depending on the nature of the malfunction

Have the vehicle inspected by a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.

**The warning light blinks** at very short intervals (0.2 seconds) in the following situations — this is normal operation, not a malfunction:
- Traction control system activates when starting or accelerating
- Stability control system activates when making a sharp turn or sudden manoeuvre
- ABS activates (on hybrid system vehicles)

When the ignition mode is changed to ON, the light comes on for approximately 2 seconds then goes off — this is the self-check and is normal.

> **NOTE:** You may also hear an operation sound or clicking sound during the self-check when pressing the engine switch to ON, when releasing the brake pedal after the ignition is turned on with the pedal depressed, or when starting the engine. This is normal.

> **CAUTION:** To ensure ESP® operates correctly, observe the following:
> - Keep tyre pressure at the specified level
> - When replacing tyres, use the same size, type, and tread pattern on all four wheels
> - Do not use excessively worn tyres
> - Do not modify the suspension or brakes, or alter vehicle height
> - Do not drive with severely deteriorated suspension or brakes
> - Do not modify the engine or exhaust
> - Do not install a limited slip differential (LSD)
> - Snow chains may cause ESP® to not operate properly

## ESP® OFF Switch
In most situations, ESP® should remain on. Use the ESP® OFF switch only in the following circumstances:
- When placing the vehicle on a tester for automobile inspection (traction control and stability control must be deactivated)
- When the vehicle is stuck and you need to rock it free (traction control may otherwise reduce power unhelpfully)

To deactivate, press and hold the ESP® OFF switch until the ESP® OFF indicator light comes on. Traction control and stability control will not work while this light is on.

To reactivate, press the ESP® OFF switch again, or stop and restart the engine. Note that traction control and stability control do not re-engage automatically after freeing a stuck vehicle — you must press the switch again (or restart the engine).

> **NOTE:** ABS and brake assist are never deactivated by the ESP® OFF switch, for safety reasons.

> **CAUTION:** If the warning light blinks at very short intervals, there is a high possibility the vehicle will skid or become stuck on a slippery surface. Drive very carefully.

## ESP® OFF Indicator Light
This light comes on when the ESP® OFF switch is pressed and held. While it is on, traction control and stability control are inactive. It comes on briefly at ignition ON as a self-check.

## Hill Hold Control System
The hill hold control system helps prevent the vehicle from rolling backwards for approximately 2 seconds while you move your foot from the brake pedal to the accelerator when starting on an uphill gradient.

> **WARNING:** The hill hold control system is not designed to hold the vehicle stationary on a hill. If you release the brake pedal for more than 2 seconds, the system cancels and the vehicle may roll backwards. On very steep slopes, accelerate immediately after releasing the brake. Do not rely excessively on this system — it may not work on excessively steep slopes, frozen roads, or mud tracks.

### Activation Conditions
The hill hold control activates to hold the vehicle for approximately 2 seconds when all of the following are met at the moment the brake pedal is released:
- The gearshift lever is in a forward or reverse gear
- The parking brake is released
- The vehicle is on an upward slope
- The brake pedal was firmly depressed

> **NOTE:** When the hill hold control activates, you may hear a sound from the engine compartment or feel the brake pedal stiffen slightly. This is normal.

If there is a problem with the hill hold control system, the ESP® warning light may come on. Consult a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
