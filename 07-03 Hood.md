# Hood
## Opening the Hood
> **WARNING:**
> - Do not allow a child to open or close the hood, as the hood is heavy and may cause injury.
> - When opening the hood, do not allow a child near it, as the engine compartment becomes hot.
> - Do not leave any tools or cloths in the engine compartment. The engine becomes hot and this may cause a fire.

> **CAUTION:** Do not come close to a running drive belt or cooling fan. Hands, hair, and clothing may be caught, causing injury. Keep away from the drive belt and cooling fan while the engine is running.

1. Pull up the hood release lever located under the driver's side instrument panel on the outboard side. This disengages the hood lock halfway.
2. Push the under-hood release lever sideways with your finger and lift up the hood.

> **CAUTION:** The under-hood release lever can be hot enough to burn your finger while the engine is running or right after it has stopped. Wait for it to cool before touching.

3. While holding the hood, pull the prop rod out from its holding clip and insert the end of the rod securely into the designated hole.

> **CAUTION:**
> - The prop rod can be hot enough to burn your finger while the engine is running or right after it has stopped. Wait for it to cool before touching.
> - Insert the end of the prop rod into the hole securely. If the prop rod slips out, you could be caught by the closing hood.
> - Be careful on windy days, as the prop rod may slip out if the hood is blown by wind.

> **NOTICE:** In snow regions, snow may become trapped in the hood latch, causing the hood open-close indicator warning light to respond slowly. Wait a few minutes and then check the indicator.
## Closing the Hood
> **NOTICE:** Do not lift up the hood with the wiper arms raised, as this can damage both the wiper arms and the hood.

1. While holding the hood, pull the prop rod out of its hole and return it to the holding clip.
2. Lower the hood to about 20 cm above the hood latch, then let it drop down.
3. Check that the hood is securely latched after closing.

> **WARNING:** If the hood is not fully closed, it can fly up unexpectedly during driving, obstructing your view and causing an accident. Always check that the hood is fully closed and latched before driving.
