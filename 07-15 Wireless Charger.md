# Wireless Charger
The wireless charger supports smartphones that are Qi 1.3 certified (see the Wireless Power Consortium website for a certified product list). It uses inductive charging technology, which is less efficient than wired charging and may cause the device to run warmer than usual.
## Using the Wireless Charger
1. Place your Qi-certified smartphone at the centre of the charging area with its charging surface facing down.
2. Close all doors (including the tailgate) and change the ignition to ACC or ON to activate the charger.
3. Keep the charging area completely clean and free of foreign objects such as coins, metal, paper, pins, or dust — between the phone, the charger surface, and the air vent.
4. For best results, remove the phone cover or case before charging.

> **NOTE:**
> - Do not operate your smartphone while charging and driving.
> - Do not use the wireless charger with a smartphone larger than the charging area.
> - Do not spill liquid on the charger or clean it with any liquid or cleaning agent.
> - Do not bring precision instruments such as wristwatches close to the charger, as they may malfunction.
> - Do not use the wireless charger for extended periods with the engine off, as prolonged charging in this condition may impact battery health.
> - If your device becomes unusually hot while charging, remove it from the charger immediately.
> - Do not place external objects on the charger or spill liquid on it or the phone, as this may cause fire, equipment failure, or burns.
> - Do not bring magnetic cards (such as credit cards) or magnetic recording media close to the charger while it is in use — data may be erased due to magnetic interference.
> - The wireless charger supports only one smartphone at a time.
> - Charging performance may vary by phone type and background apps. Charging will resume automatically once the temperature returns to the operating range.
## Indicator Light Status

| Indicator Light | Status |
|---|---|
| Off | Wireless charger off |
| Amber | Charger active and ready to charge |
| Blue | Charging in progress |
| Green | Charging complete |

> **NOTE:** Depending on the smartphone, the indicator may not turn green even after charging is complete. Indicator intensity may also vary with weather or vehicle conditions.
## Error / Malfunction Indicators

| Indicator Light | Cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Red blinking | Foreign metallic object present; phone displaced from charging area; phone or charger temperature too high | Remove the foreign object or phone cover (if it contains metal); realign the phone at the centre; or remove the phone and allow it to cool |
| Red permanent | Non-recoverable failure | Contact a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop |

## Wireless Charger Operating Conditions

| Vehicle State | Wireless Charger |
|---|---|
| Engine off, ACC off | Off |
| Engine off, ACC on | On |
| Engine on | On |
| Any door (including tailgate) open | Off; turns on once all doors are closed |
| Vehicle battery too low | Off |
| Key fob taken outside the vehicle | Off; turns on once key fob is back inside |

> **NOTICE:** Do not charge your devices for extended periods when the ignition is in ACC mode or the engine is off, as the battery may discharge.

> **NOTE:**
> - Phones with a magnetic array for charger alignment may experience varying charging performance and heat levels.
> - The charger may not function correctly if the smartphone overheats, other wireless keys are nearby, or there are other unspecified issues.
> - Charging performance may be affected near TV towers, electric power plants, gas stations, large displays, airports, or other sources of strong radio waves or electrical noise.
> - If your smartphone has a protruding camera, try adjusting its position on the charger to improve charging performance.
> - Consult your physician before using the wireless charger if you have any implantable medical device such as a cardiac pacemaker, as it may affect the device's operation.
