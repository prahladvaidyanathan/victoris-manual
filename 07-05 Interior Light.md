# Interior Light
Your vehicle has several interior lights: footwell lights, front and center overhead lights, a luggage compartment light, door ambient lights, and a vanity mirror light.
## Footwell Lights
A footwell light is provided in the leg space in front of each front seat. The light comes on when a door is opened. After all doors are closed, it remains on for about 15 seconds and then fades out. If the ignition is changed to ACC or ON during this time, the light begins to fade out immediately. After changing the ignition to LOCK (OFF), the light turns on for about 15 seconds and then fades out.

> **NOTE:** When the ignition is LOCK (OFF) and a door is left open, the light will automatically turn off after about 15 minutes to prevent battery discharge.
## Instrument Panel Assistant Ornament Ambient Light (if equipped)
This light illuminates the design patterns on the panel on the front passenger seat side.

- When you unlock the door, the light comes on for 15 seconds.
- When you open the door, the light remains on for 15 minutes.
- With the ignition in LOCK (OFF), if you close the door, the light turns on for 15 seconds and then fades out.
- When all doors are closed, the ignition is ON, and exterior lights are off, the light comes on at 100% brightness.
- When all doors are closed, the ignition is ON, and exterior lights are on, the brightness can be adjusted.
- When the ignition is ON, you can turn the light off/on, change the colour, and adjust brightness.

> **NOTE:** You can change the ambient light settings from the Ambient Lighting screen on the infotainment unit.
## Front Interior Light
The front interior light switch (located on the light unit itself) has the following positions:

**ON:** The light stays on regardless of whether a door is open or closed.

**DOOR:** The light comes on when a door is opened. After all doors are closed, it remains on for about 15 seconds and then fades out. If the ignition is changed to ACC or ON during this time, the light begins to fade immediately. After the ignition is changed to LOCK (OFF), the light turns on for about 15 seconds and fades out.

**OFF:** The light remains off even when a door is opened.

**Spot light:** When the switch is in DOOR or OFF position, press the switch to turn on the spot light. Press again to turn it off.

> **NOTE:**
> - The light will turn off automatically after a set period when left illuminated, to prevent battery discharge.
> - The light will automatically turn off when all of the following are true: the ignition is LOCK (OFF), the hazard warning lights, position lights, and headlights are all off, and 15 minutes have passed since the light was turned on.
> - For a short while after the ignition is changed to LOCK (OFF), the light may not come on even if the switch is turned on. This is normal behavior to prevent battery discharge.
## Center Light
The center interior light has a push-type switch. Press to turn on; press again to turn off.

> **NOTE:** When the ignition is LOCK (OFF) and a door is left open, the light will automatically turn off after about 15 minutes to prevent battery discharge.
## Luggage Compartment Light (if equipped)
When the tailgate is opened with the luggage compartment light switch in the ON position, the light comes on for about 15 minutes. When the switch is in the OFF position, the light remains off regardless of whether the tailgate is open or closed.

> **NOTE:** To prevent battery discharge, do not leave the luggage compartment light on longer than necessary.
## Door Ambient Light (if equipped)
A door ambient light illuminates the door handle area inside the vehicle.

- When you unlock the door, the light comes on for 15 seconds.
- When you open the door, the light remains on for 15 minutes.
- With the ignition in LOCK (OFF), if you close the door, the light turns on for 15 seconds and then fades out.
- When all doors are closed, the ignition is ON, and exterior lights are off, the light comes on at 100% brightness.
- When all doors are closed, the ignition is ON, and exterior lights are on, the brightness can be adjusted.
- When the ignition is ON, you can turn the light off/on, change the colour, and adjust brightness.

> **NOTE:** You can change the door ambient light settings from the Ambient Lighting screen on the infotainment unit.
## Vanity Mirror Light (if equipped)
The vanity mirror light comes on when the mirror cover on the sun visor is opened.

> **NOTE:** To prevent battery discharge, do not leave the vanity mirror light on for extended periods.
