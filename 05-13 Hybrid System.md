# Hybrid System (if equipped)
For details, refer to [[02-00 For Safe Driving]] for hybrid system features.

## Energy Flow Indicator
With the energy flow indicator in the instrument cluster, you can check the energy flow among the engine, the lithium-ion battery and the tyres.

Each colour indicates the following information:
- Orange: Energy flow of the engine
- Blue: Energy flow between the tyres and the motor
- White: Energy flow between the battery and the motor

The following energy flow states may be displayed:
- No energy flow.
- The vehicle is running by the energy of the lithium-ion battery. (Blue and white)
- The vehicle is running by the energy of both the engine and the lithium-ion battery. (Blue, orange and white)
- The vehicle is running by the energy of the engine. (Orange)
- The vehicle is running and the battery is being charged by the energy of the engine. (Orange and white)
- The lithium-ion battery is being charged by the energy of the engine. (Orange and white)
- The lithium-ion battery is being charged by the deceleration energy regeneration function. (Blue and white)

## Battery Indicator
The segments in the battery indicator give an approximate indication of the charge of the lithium-ion battery.
