# Seat Belt

> **NOTE:** For information on children and seat belts, refer to [[02-08 Safety for Child]].
## Information for Pregnant Women and People with Medical Conditions

> **WARNING:**
> - Those that are pregnant or sick/disabled must also wear their seat belts. However, in the event of a collision, there is the danger of heavy pressure focused on certain body areas. Consultation with a physician is recommended.
> - For those that are pregnant, ensure that the lap portion of the belt is as low as possible across the pelvis, avoiding touching the abdomen. Make sure the shoulder strap rests halfway between the shoulder and the base of the neck, crossing the chest and avoiding touching the abdomen.
## Lap-Shoulder Belt
### Emergency Locking Retractor (ELR)

The seat belt has an emergency locking retractor (ELR), which is designed to lock the seat belt only during a sudden stop or impact. It also may lock if you pull the belt across your body very quickly. If this happens, let the belt go back to unlock it, then pull the belt across your body more slowly.
### Fastening and Unfastening

To fasten the seat belt, sit up straight and far back into the seat. Pull the latch plate across your body and press it straight into the buckle until you hear a click. After pressing the latch plate into the buckle, pull the seat belt to make sure it is securely latched.

To unfasten the seat belt, push the red "PRESS" button on the buckle and retract the belt slowly while holding the belt and/or the latch plate.

> **NOTE:**
> - If the seat belt cannot be pulled from its fully retracted position, firmly pull the belt and release it, then smoothly pull the belt out of the retractor.
> - The word "CENTER" is marked on the buckle for the rear centre belt. The buckles are designed so that a latch plate cannot be inserted into the wrong buckle.

> **WARNING:** To minimise the risk of severe injury or death in the event of a collision, always wear the seat belt with both latches buckled.
## Rear Centre Lap-Shoulder Belt with Detachable Connector

The rear centre lap-shoulder belt has a buckle, a latch plate, and a detachable connector. The buckle is marked "CENTER" to distinguish it from the rear left seat belt buckle. The buckle and connector are designed so that a wrong latch plate cannot be inserted.

When the rear seatback is in the upright position, keep the detachable connector latched. Only unlatch the detachable connector when the rear seatback will be folded down.

**Fastening:** Before fastening the rear centre lap-shoulder belt, make sure the detachable connector (1) is securely latched and the webbing is not twisted. Sit up straight and well back in the seat, pull the latch plate (2) across your body and press it straight into the buckle (3) until you hear a click.

**Unfastening:** Push the button on the buckle (3) and retract the belt slowly while holding the belt and/or the latch plate.
### Unlatching and Latching the Detachable Connector

**Unlatching:**
1. Insert a latch plate into the slot (1) on the connector and allow the belt to retract.
2. Once the belt has completely retracted, stow the belt in the holder (see Stowing the Rear Centre Lap-Shoulder Belt below).

> **NOTICE:** When the rear seatback is folded forward, be sure to unlatch the connector of the rear centre lap-shoulder belt. Otherwise, the seat belt webbing can be damaged.

**Latching:**
1. Pull the belt out from the holder.
2. Insert the connector latch plate (2) into the connector (3) until you hear a click.

> **WARNING:** Make sure the detachable connector is securely latched and the webbing is not twisted.
### Stowing the Rear Centre Lap-Shoulder Belt

Stow the belt after it is completely retracted. To stow the belt, insert the latch plate into the slit (1), then insert the connector latch plate (2) into the roof holder slot (3).
## Seat Belt Length Adjustment

The seat belt does not require length adjustment. It extends and retracts according to the movement of the body. If the seat belt receives a strong impact, the belt locks its tension automatically to secure the body.
## Correct Use of Seat Belt

> **WARNING:**
> - Use the seat belt correctly. Otherwise, in a sudden braking or a collision, the body may not be restrained, resulting in severe injury.
> - If the seat belt is twisted or contains excessive slack, there is a danger of heavy pressure focused on certain body areas.
> - If the seat belt rests on the abdomen, this area may sustain heavy pressure in a strong impact.
> - If the seat belt does not rest firmly over the shoulder, you may be thrown forward in a strong impact.
## Seat Belt Reminder

The seat belt reminder indicators in the instrument cluster are:
- **(1)** Driver's and front passenger's seat belt reminder light
- **(2)** Rear passenger's seat belt reminder light (rear left)
- **(3)** Rear passenger's seat belt reminder light (rear centre)
- **(4)** Rear passenger's seat belt reminder light (rear right)

> **WARNING:** It is absolutely essential that the driver, front passenger, and all rear passengers fasten their seat belts at all times. Persons who are not fastening seat belts have a much greater risk of injury if an accident occurs. Make a regular habit of buckling your seat belt before starting the engine.

> **NOTE:**
> - Along with the seat belt reminder light, a reminder message will also appear on the information display for an unrestrained driver and/or front passenger.
> - The driver's seat belt reminder light (1) covers both the driver and front passenger.
### Driver's Seat Belt Reminder

If the driver's seat belt remains unbuckled when the ignition mode is changed to ON, the reminder works as follows:

1. The driver's seat belt reminder light comes on.
2. After the vehicle's speed reaches about 15 km/h, the reminder light blinks and a buzzer sounds for about 95 seconds.
3. The reminder light remains on until the driver's seat belt is buckled.

If the driver buckles the seat belt and then later unbuckles it, the reminder activates from Step 1 or Step 2 depending on vehicle speed: below 15 km/h it starts from Step 1; above 15 km/h it starts from Step 2.

The reminder is automatically cancelled when the driver's seat belt is buckled or the ignition mode is changed to LOCK (OFF).
### Front Passenger's Seat Belt Reminder

If a person is sitting in the front passenger's seat and the front passenger's seat belt is unbuckled when the ignition mode is changed to ON, the front passenger's seat belt reminder activates in the same manner as the driver's seat belt reminder.

The seat belt reminder sensor detects whether a person is sitting in the front seat. The sensor is located in the seat cushion.

> **NOTICE:**
> - The sensor of the front passenger's seat belt reminder is located in the seat cushion. If heavy or sharp objects are placed on the seat cushion, or if the passenger's seat is removed, disassembled, or modified, the sensor may not work properly or may be damaged. Do not put heavy or sharp objects on the seat cushion. Do not remove, disassemble, or modify the passenger's seat.
> - Depending on the type of seat cover used, operation of the sensor may be adversely affected. Maruti Suzuki highly recommends using genuine Maruti Suzuki seat covers. If a non-genuine seat cover is installed, consult a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
> - If you spill liquid such as soft drinks or juice on the front passenger's seat cushion, the sensor can be damaged. Immediately wipe it dry with a soft cloth.
> - When sitting on the front passenger's seat, it is very important that the passenger sits upright, leaning against the seat backrest and centred on the seat cushion for the seat belt reminder system to function effectively. An occupant sitting improperly (slouching, turning sideways, sitting forward or sideways) may hamper the functioning of this system as it may not detect the occupant.

> **NOTE:**
> - If you place an object on the passenger's seat, the weight of the object will be sensed by the sensor and the front passenger's seat belt reminder light may come on and the interior buzzer may beep.
> - If a child or a small-sized person sits on the front passenger's seat, or if a cushion is placed on the seat, the weight may not be sensed by the sensor and the interior buzzer may not beep.
### Rear Passenger's Seat Belt Reminder

When the ignition mode is turned ON, the rear seat belt reminder works as follows:

1. The reminder light turns ON for non-buckled seats and continues for vehicle speed below 15 km/h.
2. When vehicle speed is above 15 km/h, the reminder light remains ON for the non-buckled seats for about 65 seconds and then turns OFF.
3. When a buckled seat belt is subsequently unbuckled, the corresponding reminder light turns ON. Above 15 km/h, the reminder light blinks (about 65 seconds) along with a buzzer sound (about 35 seconds). The reminder light remains ON until the seat belt is buckled.

If a rear door is opened while the vehicle is stationary, the reminder system starts from Step 1. The reminder is automatically cancelled when all rear seat belts are buckled or the ignition mode is changed to LOCK (OFF).
