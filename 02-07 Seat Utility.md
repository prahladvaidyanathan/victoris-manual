# Seat Utility
## Folding Rear Seats

The rear seats can be folded forward to provide additional cargo space.

To fold the rear seats forward:

1. Lower the head restraint fully.
2. Move the outboard lap-shoulder belts to the rear door side trims.
3. Slide the release lever (1) on the top of each split seat in the arrow direction and fold the seatbacks forward.

> **WARNING:** If you need to carry cargo in the passenger compartment with the seatback folded forward, secure the cargo or it may be thrown about, causing injury. Never pile cargo higher than the seatbacks.

> **NOTICE:**
> - After folding the rear seatback forward, do not allow any foreign material to enter the lock opening. This may cause damage to the inside of the lock and prevent the seatback from being locked securely when returned.
> - When moving a seatback, make sure the seat belts are placed on the rear door side trims so the seat belts and buckles are not caught by the seatback, seat hinge, or seat latch. This helps prevent damage to the belt system.
> - Make sure the belt webbing is not twisted.
## Returning the Seat to Normal Position

Raise the seatback until it locks into place. After returning the seat, move the seatback back and forth to check that it is securely latched.

> **CAUTION:**
> - When returning the rear seatback to the normal position, be careful that your fingers are not caught between the lock and the striker.
> - Do not put your hand into the rear seatback lock opening, or your finger may get caught and be injured.

> **CAUTION:** After securing the rear seatback, check that it is locked securely. If it is not, the red indicator (2) will appear beside the release lever (1).

> **NOTICE:**
> - When returning the rear seatback to the normal position, do not allow any foreign material to enter the lock opening. This may prevent the seatback from being locked securely.
> - Handle the seatback carefully by hand to avoid any damage to the lock itself. Do not push it using an object or by applying excessive force.
> - The lock is designed exclusively for securing the rear seatback. Do not use it for any other purpose, as incorrect use may cause damage to the inside of the lock.
> - Before returning the seatback, check that there is nothing around the striker. Any foreign material may prevent the seatback from locking securely.
