# Brakes
## General Braking
Braking distance increases significantly with speed — for example, the distance needed to stop from 60 km/h is approximately four times that needed from 30 km/h. Begin braking early and slow down gradually.

> **NOTE:** You may hear operational noise from friction between the brake disc and brake lining during braking. This is normal and does not indicate a malfunction.

> **WARNING:** If water gets into the brake components (e.g. after driving through a puddle or washing the underside of the vehicle), braking performance may become poor or unpredictable. Test the brakes at slow speed afterward. If effectiveness is reduced, dry the brakes by applying them repeatedly while driving slowly until normal braking performance is restored.

## Power-assisted Brakes
The vehicle is equipped with power-assisted brakes. If power assistance is lost (e.g. due to a stalled engine or other failure), the system retains reserve power and you can still bring the vehicle to a full stop by pressing the brake pedal once and holding it down firmly. Reserve power is partially consumed with each brake application, so do not pump the pedal — apply smooth, even pressure.

> **WARNING:** Even without reserve power, you can still stop the vehicle by pressing the brake pedal harder than normal. However, stopping distance will be longer.

> **NOTE:** You may hear operational sounds during brake pedal application and release due to components in the brake system activating. This is normal and does not affect braking function.

## Brake Assist System
When emergency braking is detected (i.e. the brake pedal is pressed quickly and forcefully), the brake assist system supplements braking force for drivers who cannot hold the pedal down firmly enough.

> **NOTE:** A clicking sound when quickly and forcefully depressing the brake pedal is normal and indicates the brake assist system is working correctly. On hybrid vehicles, you may also hear operating sounds from the brake system in the following situations — none of these indicate a malfunction:
> - Sounds from the engine compartment when the brake pedal is operated
> - A motor sound from the front of the vehicle when the driver's door is opened
> - Sounds from the engine compartment one or two minutes after the hybrid system is stopped

## Anti-lock Brake System (ABS)
ABS prevents wheel lock-up by electronically controlling braking pressure, helping you maintain steering control during hard braking or on slippery surfaces. The system operates automatically — no special braking technique is required. Press the brake pedal firmly and hold it; do not pump. The ABS will activate whenever it detects wheels locking up.

> **WARNING:** ABS has limits and may not function properly in all conditions. It cannot overcome situations where tyre grip is exceeded or hydroplaning occurs.

> **WARNING:** ABS uses wheel rotation sensors to function. Using tyres other than those specified (wrong size, type, tread pattern, or excessively worn) will prevent correct wheel speed detection and ABS will not work properly, possibly leading to an accident. Always use manufacturer-specified tyres and replace them before they become excessively worn.

### What to Expect During ABS Operation
- You may feel the brake pedal pulsate or move slightly — this is normal and indicates brake fluid pressure is being correctly controlled.
- You may feel vibration in the brake pedal, steering wheel, and vehicle body during hard braking — continue pressing firmly.
- A clunking noise during ABS activation is normal.
- ABS does not activate below approximately 9 km/h (varies with road conditions).
- On hybrid vehicles, the ESP® warning light blinks while ABS is operating — this is normal.

You may also hear a motor sound briefly after starting the engine or beginning to drive. This is the system running a self-check and is not a malfunction.

### Braking Distance with ABS
ABS is not designed to shorten stopping distance. On slippery roads or when braking suddenly, stopping distance with ABS is approximately the same as without. For the shortest possible stopping distance, press the brake pedal firmly without pumping.

> **WARNING:** In the following situations, stopping distance with ABS may be slightly longer than without ABS. Drive slowly and maintain extra following distance:
> - Unpaved or stone-paved roads
> - Gravel roads or fresh snow
> - Road joints and surface height differences
> - Metal surfaces such as manhole covers
> - When snow chains are installed

### Situations Where ABS May Activate Unexpectedly
ABS may activate briefly when braking on the following surfaces, which is normal:
- Slippery surfaces (manholes, metal plates in construction zones, gravel)
- Road joints and height differences in the road surface
- Bumpy roads and cobblestones

### ABS Warning Light
If there is a problem with the ABS system when the ignition is in ON mode, this light comes on. Have the vehicle inspected by a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.

## Braking Assist
Braking assist provides additional braking force when the brake pedal is operated. When sudden braking is applied, it shortens the time for ABS to reach full effectiveness.

- Braking assist will not operate if the brake pedal is not depressed firmly.
- Braking assist does not extend braking performance beyond the vehicle's inherent capability.
