# Hazard Warning Switch
Use the hazard warning switch to warn other drivers of a possible traffic hazard when parking in an emergency.

Push the hazard warning switch to activate the hazard warning lights — all turn signal lights and their indicators on the instrument cluster will flash simultaneously. Push the switch again to turn them off.

> **NOTICE:** Using the hazard warning switch for many hours with the engine stopped may drain the battery. Do not use the hazard warning switch for extended periods with the engine stopped.
