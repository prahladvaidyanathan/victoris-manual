# Keys
Your vehicle comes with a pair of identical keys. Keep one key as a spare in a safe place. One key can open all of the locks on the vehicle.
## Key Number Plate
The key identification number (1) is stamped on a metal tag (2) provided with the keys. Keep the tag in a safe place. If you lose your keys, you will need this number to have new keys made.

> **NOTE:**
> - Carefully keep the tag in a place other than your vehicle, so that someone cannot find out about the key identification number. If you lose your key, inform the key identification number to a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
> - When you resell or transfer your vehicle to a new owner or operator, give the key number plate with your vehicle to them.
