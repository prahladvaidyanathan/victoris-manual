# Service Checklist — 5,000 km (6 months)
This is a **Free Inspection** service. Perform all items below, whichever comes first — 5,000 km or 6 months.

**Key:** I = Inspect / C = Clean / T = Tighten / O = Rotate

## Engine
- [ ] **I** Engine oil, oil filter & drain plug gasket (level, leakage)
- [ ] **I** Cooling system hoses & connections (leakage, damage)
- [ ] **I** Exhaust system (noise, leakage)
- [ ] **I** Positive crankcase ventilation system — hoses, connections & valve

## Fuel
- [ ] **C** Air cleaner filter element — clean every 5,000 km (paved road)
- [ ] **I** Fuel tank cap, fuel lines & connections (leakage, damage)

## Clutch & Transmission
- [ ] **I** Clutch fluid (level, leakage)
- [ ] **I** Clutch slipping (dragging or excess damage)
- [ ] **I** Manual transmission oil (level, leakage)
- [ ] **I** Transfer oil — 4WD (level, leakage)
- [ ] **I** Rear differential oil — 4WD (level, leakage)
- [ ] **I** Hose, automatic transaxle cooler (leakage, damage)
- [ ] **I** Gear shifter (operation)

## Drive Shaft
- [ ] **I** Drive shaft noise
- [ ] **I** Drive shaft boot / propeller shafts (boot damage)

## Brake
- [ ] **I** Brake fluid (level, leakage)
- [ ] **I** Brake pedal — wall clearance
- [ ] **I** Parking brake lever & cable (play, damage)
- [ ] **I** Brake disc & pad (wear)
- [ ] **I** Brake hoses & pipes (fluid leakage, damage)
- [ ] **I** Master cylinder, wheel cylinder & caliper piston (fluid leakage, boot/seal damage)

## Wheel
- [ ] **I&O** Tyres (air pressure, abnormal wear, cracks & rotation)
- [ ] **I** Wheels (damage)
- [ ] **I** Front/rear wheel bearing (loose, damage)

## Front / Rear Suspension
- [ ] **I** Suspension strut (oil leakage, damage)
- [ ] **I** Suspension arms / knuckle support & torsion rods (loose, damage)
- [ ] **I** Rear spring (damage)
- [ ] **I** Shock absorbers (oil leakage, damage)
- [ ] **T** All bolts & nuts (loose)

## Steering
- [ ] **I** Steering wheel (play, loose)
- [ ] **I** All rods & arms (loose, damage, wear)
- [ ] **I** Tilt & telescopic steering (operation)

## Electrical
- [ ] **I** Battery — electrolyte (level, leakage) & voltage
- [ ] **I** Wiring harness connection (loose, damage)
- [ ] **I** Lighting system (operation, stains, damage)
- [ ] **I** Wiper (operation, stains, damage)
- [ ] **I** Horn (operation)
- [ ] **I** USB & accessory socket (operation)
- [ ] **I** Sunroof (operation)
- [ ] **I** Wireless charger

## Body
- [ ] **T** All chassis bolts & nuts
- [ ] **I&L** All latches, hinges & locks (function)

## Road Test
- [ ] **I** Operation of brakes, gear shifting & speedometer
- [ ] **I** Body & chassis noise

## Air Conditioner
- [ ] **T** Compressor mounting bolt
- [ ] **I** All hose joints (check, tighten)
- [ ] **I** Functioning of recirculating flap
- [ ] **C** Condenser — clean with low-pressure water
- [ ] **I** All mounting bolts
- [ ] **I** AC filter element (standard)
