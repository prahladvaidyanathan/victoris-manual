# Windows
## Electric Window Controls
The electric windows can only be operated when the ignition mode is ON.

The driver's door has a switch (1) to operate the driver's window, a switch (2) to operate the front passenger's window, and switches (3) and (4) to operate the rear right and left passenger windows respectively.

The passenger's door has a switch (5) to operate the passenger's window.

To open a window, push the top part of the switch. To close the window, lift up the top part of the switch.

The driver's window has AUTO-DOWN and AUTO-UP features. Press the driver's window switch completely down or up and release it — the window will travel to the fully open or fully closed position without holding the switch. To stop the window before it reaches the end, briefly push or pull the switch in the opposite direction.

> **NOTE:** If you drive with one of the rear windows open, you may hear a loud sound caused by air vibration. To reduce the sound, open the driver's or front passenger's window, or narrow the rear window opening.
## Lock Switch
The driver's door also has a lock switch for the passenger's window(s). When you push in the lock switch, the passenger's window(s) cannot be raised or lowered by operating any of the switches (2), (3), (4) or (5). To restore normal operation, push the lock switch again.

> **WARNING:**
> - When opening or closing a window, a part of the body such as hands, legs or neck may be caught and an accident may occur. Observe the following:
>   - Both the driver and other passengers should be careful when opening or closing a window.
>   - Do not allow a child to operate the window switches.
>   - Always lock the passenger's window operation when there are children in the vehicle.
>   - When you leave your vehicle, press the engine/power switch to change the ignition mode to OFF, carry your key, and take children out of the vehicle.
>   - When operating the front passenger's or rear windows from the driver's side, check that other passengers do not have their hands or faces outside the vehicle, and inform them before operating.
> - Do not operate the electric window controls from outside the window. Your hands or neck may be caught.
## Pinching Prevention Function
The driver's window is equipped with a pinching prevention function. When closing the window using the AUTO-UP feature, if a foreign object is detected caught in the window, the window automatically stops closing to prevent damage.

> **WARNING:** The function may not detect an object depending on its shape, size, hardness and position. This may cause severe injury. Be very careful when opening or closing the windows.

> **CAUTION:**
> - The pinching prevention function does not operate while you are holding the window switch in the UP position.
> - The pinching prevention function may not detect an object caught in the window just before it is fully closed.

> **NOTE:**
> - Even if the window cannot be closed by AUTO-UP due to a possible fault with the pinching prevention function, you can still close the window by holding the switch in the UP position.
> - If you drive in extreme off-road conditions, the pinching prevention function may operate accidentally due to vehicle jolting.
### Initialising the Pinching Prevention Function
When you disconnect and reconnect the battery or replace a fuse, the pinching prevention function will be deactivated. In this condition, the AUTO-DOWN feature will be deactivated while AUTO-UP may remain active. The function must be re-initialised before use.

> **WARNING:** The pinching prevention function will not operate until initialisation is completed. Make sure to perform the initialisation.

To initialise:
1. Press the engine/power switch to change the ignition mode to ON.
2. Open the driver's window fully by holding the window switch in the DOWN position.
3. Close the driver's window by holding the switch in the UP position, and keep holding it for 2 seconds after the window is fully closed.
4. Check that the AUTO-DOWN/UP feature works correctly.

If the AUTO-DOWN/UP feature does not work after initialisation, there may be a fault with the pinching prevention function. Have your vehicle inspected by a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
