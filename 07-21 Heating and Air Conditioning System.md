# Heating and Air Conditioning System
Your vehicle has the following air outlets: windshield defroster outlet, side defroster outlet, side outlet, center outlet, floor outlet, and rear outlet.
## Adjusting the Center Outlet
Move the knob on the center outlet vertically or horizontally to adjust the direction of airflow.
## Adjusting the Side and Rear Outlets
Move the direction knob vertically or horizontally to adjust the airflow direction. Use the dial to open or close the outlet:
- **Side outlet:** Turn the dial upward to open; downward to close.
- **Rear outlet:** Move the dial to the right to open; to the left to close.

When open, air comes out of the side and rear outlets regardless of the air flow selector position.

> **CAUTION:** Prolonged exposure to hot air from the heater or air conditioner could result in low-temperature burns. All vehicle occupants — particularly children, the elderly, those with special needs, individuals with delicate skin, and sleeping individuals — should maintain sufficient distance from the air outlets.

> **NOTE:**
> - To quickly clear fog from the front door windows, direct airflow from the side outlets toward them.
> - Airflow from the rear outlet can be increased by fully moving the center outlet knob to the inner side.
