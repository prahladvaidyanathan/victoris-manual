# Gear Oil
## Specified Oils
| Application | Recommended oil | Viscosity range |
|-------------|----------------|----------------|
| Manual transmission | MARUTI GENUINE GEAR OIL 75W | 75W (−30°C to 40°C) |
| Transfer oil — 4WD | SUZUKI GEAR OIL 75W-85 | 75W-85 (−30°C to 40°C) |
| Rear differential oil — 4WD | SUZUKI GEAR OIL 75W-85 | 75W-85 (−30°C to 40°C) |

## Maintenance Schedule — K15C Engine
| Item | 1k | 5k | 10k | 20k | 30k | 40k | 50k | 60k | 70k | 80k |
|------|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Manual transmission oil (level, leakage) | I | I | I | I | I | I | I | I | I | I |
| Transfer oil — 4WD (level, leakage) | I | I | I | I | I | I | I | I | I | I |
| Rear differential oil — 4WD (level, leakage) | I | I | I | I | I | I | I | I | I | I |

**Transfer oil and rear differential oil (4WD):** Replace every 150,000 km or 120 months, whichever comes first.

## Service
Gear oil level check and oil changes require special procedures, materials, and tools. Have all gear oil work done at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
