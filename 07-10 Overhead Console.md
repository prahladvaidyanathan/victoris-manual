# Overhead Console
The overhead console provides storage for small articles. Open the lid by pressing the raised portion on it. Always close the lid after putting something in or taking something out.

> **WARNING:**
> - Do not drive with the overhead console lid open. Articles inside could fall during braking, acceleration, or in a collision.
> - If you park outdoors in direct sunlight or hot weather, the overhead console can get very hot. Do not leave eyeglasses or plastic cards inside — the heat may distort plastic lenses or frames. Do not place flammable items such as a lighter inside — the heat may cause a fire.

> **CAUTION:** Do not store heavy or large articles. The lid may open from impact during driving, causing contents to fly out and potentially damaging the console.

> **NOTICE:**
> - Do not apply undue force when closing the lid, as this could break the lid and damage articles inside.
> - Place glasses in a case before storing them, or the lenses may be scratched.
## Coat Hooks
Coat hooks are located alongside the rear right assist grip. You can hang clothing on them. These hooks are not designed for large or heavy items.

> **WARNING:** Your vehicle is equipped with side curtain airbags. Do not hang sharp items such as a hanger on the coat hook. When hanging clothes, hang them directly on the hook without using a hanger.

> **NOTICE:** Do not hang items heavier than 2 kg on the coat hook, as this may break it.
