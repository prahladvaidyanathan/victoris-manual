# Engine Oil and Filter
## Specified Oil — K15C Engine (India)
**Recommended grade:** SAE 0W-16 (preferred — best for fuel economy and cold-weather starting)

**Quality classification:** API SN or SP

Maruti Suzuki genuine oil is recommended. To purchase it, contact a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.

## Oil Level Check
Check the oil level with the vehicle on a level surface. The reading will be inaccurate if the vehicle is on a slope.

Check either **before starting the engine** or at least **5 minutes after stopping** the engine.

1. Pull out the oil dipstick and wipe it clean with a cloth.
2. Reinsert the dipstick all the way into the engine.
3. Pull it out again and read the level.
4. The oil should be between the **Upper** and **Lower** marks on the dipstick.
5. If the level is near the Lower mark, add enough oil to bring it up to the Upper mark.

> [!notice] Failure to check the oil level regularly could lead to serious engine trouble due to insufficient oil.

## Service Tasks — Workshop Only
The following require special procedures and must be performed at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop:

- Refilling with oil
- Changing engine oil
- Oil filter replacement
- Oil leakage check
