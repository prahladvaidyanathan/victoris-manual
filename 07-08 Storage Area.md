# Storage Area
Your vehicle provides the following storage areas: glove box, floor console tray (if equipped), front armrest with console box, and utility pocket (if equipped).

> **WARNING:**
> - If you use a storage area without a lid while driving, do not place objects that could fall out and obstruct the brake pedal or accelerator pedal.
> - Do not place large objects on the instrument panel tray. Objects in the path of the front passenger's airbag may be thrown out or prevent the airbag from inflating properly.

> **CAUTION:** Do not drive with a storage lid open. During acceleration, sudden braking, or a collision, your body may be struck by the lid or by objects falling from the storage area.

> **NOTE:** If you place the keyless remote controller in any storage area, it may not be detected.
## Glove Box
Pull the latch lever to open the glove box. Push the lid until it latches securely to close. The glove box light stays on while the lid is open.

> **WARNING:**
> - Never drive with the glove box lid open, as this could cause injury in an accident.
> - Do not leave cigarette lighters or spray cans in the glove box. They may ignite when luggage is loaded, causing a fire.
## Front Armrest with Console Box
The front armrest can be slid forward. To open the console box, raise the top lid while keeping the latch lever lifted up. Always close the lid after putting items in or taking items out.

> **NOTICE:** Do not lean on the armrest or allow a child to sit on it, as this may cause damage.
