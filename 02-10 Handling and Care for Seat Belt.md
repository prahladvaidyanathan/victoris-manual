# Handling and Care for Seat Belt
## Seat Belt Handling

Periodically inspect the seat belts to make sure they work properly and are not damaged. Check the webbing, buckles, latch plates, retractors, anchorages, and guide loops. Replace any seat belts which do not work properly or are damaged.

> **WARNING:**
> - Inspect all seat belt assemblies after any collision. Any seat belt assembly which was in use during a collision (other than a very minor one) should be replaced, even if damage to the assembly is not obvious. Any seat belt assembly which was not in use during a collision must also be replaced if the seat belt pretensioners were activated. The pretensioners and the load limiter function only once. If they did not activate, consult a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
> - If frays, scratches, or cuts are found on a seat belt, replace it with a new one.
> - If a buckle does not work properly, have it inspected at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
> - If strong force was applied to a seat belt in a collision, the seat belt function may be damaged even though its appearance is normal. Replace the seat belt with a new one.
> - As the seat belt may not provide best performance and may result in severe injury in an emergency, observe the following:
>   - If foreign material enters a buckle or you spill a drink on it, the seat belt may not work properly. Have it inspected at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
>   - Be careful not to get the seat belt caught in a door. Before closing the door, check that the seat belt is fully retracted without any slack.
>   - Do not modify or remove the seat belt.
## Rear Seat Belt Handling

Place the outboard lap-shoulder belts on the rear door side trims when the rear seat belts are not in use.

> **NOTICE:** When moving a seatback, make sure the seat belts are placed on the rear door side trims so the seat belts and buckles are not caught by the seatback, seat hinge, or seat latch. This helps prevent damage to the belt system.
## Seat Belt Care

Caring for seat belts is the same as caring for any type of regular cloth material.

> **WARNING:** Do not use bleach, solvent, or dyes on seat belts. Doing so may cause stains, discolouration, and strength deterioration, and the seat belt may not function properly.
## Shoulder Anchor Height Adjuster (Only for Front Seat)

Adjust the shoulder anchor height so that the shoulder belt rides on the centre of the outboard shoulder. To move the anchor up, slide it up. To move it down, slide the anchor down while pulling the lock knob out. After adjustment, check that the anchor is securely locked.

> **WARNING:** Check that the shoulder belt is positioned on the centre of the outside shoulder. The belt should be away from your face and neck, but not falling off your shoulder. Misadjustment of the belt could reduce the effectiveness of the seat belt in a collision.
