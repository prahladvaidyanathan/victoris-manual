# Door Pocket
Each door pocket is intended for holding light and soft items such as gloves, newspapers, or magazines.

> **CAUTION:**
> - Do not put hard or breakable objects such as bottles or cans in the door pocket. In an accident, these objects could injure the occupants.
> - When closing a door, do not grab and pull the door pocket, as this may break it and cause personal injury.

> **NOTE:** If you place the keyless remote controller in a door pocket, it may not be detected.
