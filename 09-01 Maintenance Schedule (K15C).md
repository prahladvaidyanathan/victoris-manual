# Maintenance Schedule — K15C Engine Model
> [!warning] Always have maintenance performed at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
> Whenever parts need to be replaced, use Maruti Suzuki genuine replacement parts or their equivalent.

The table below shows the times (by odometer reading **or** months, whichever comes first) when regular maintenance should be performed. Beyond 80,000 km, repeat the same services at the same intervals.

## Legend
| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| C | Clean |
| R | Replace or Change |
| I | Inspect, clean, adjust, lubricate or replace as necessary |
| L | Lubricate |
| T | Tighten to Specified Torque |
| O | Rotate |
| – | Not required at this interval |

The first three columns (1k / 5k / 10k km) are **Free Inspection** services. All subsequent columns are **Periodic Maintenance at Cost**.

### Engine Coolant (1-3) — Fixed Replacement Schedule
- **First time:** Replace at 100,000 km or 60 months, whichever comes first.
- **Second time and after:** Replace every 40,000 km or 24 months, whichever comes first.
- *Fluid spec: SUZUKI LLC Super (Blue)*

### Fuel Items — Fixed Replacement Schedule
- **3-1 Air cleaner filter element (paved road):** Clean every 5,000 km. Replace every 40,000 km.
- **3-1 Air cleaner filter element (dusty conditions):** Clean every 2,500 km or as required. Replace every 40,000 km.
- **3-3 Fuel filter:** Replace every 100,000 km or 120 months, whichever comes first.
