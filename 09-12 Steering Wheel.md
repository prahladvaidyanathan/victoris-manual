## Steering Wheel Play Check
**Specified play "c": 0 – 30 mm**

To check, gently turn the steering wheel left and right and measure the distance it moves before you feel slight resistance. The play should be within the specified range.

Also check that the steering wheel turns easily and smoothly without rattling when turned all the way to the right and left while driving very slowly in an open area.

If the free play is outside the specified range, or if anything else seems wrong, have the vehicle inspected at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
