# Acoustic Vehicle Alerting System (if equipped)
When the vehicle is driven using the electric motor (traction motor) at a speed of approximately 23 km/h or less, an alarm is sounded to alert those around the vehicle to its approach.

The alarm may be difficult for people around you to hear when there is a lot of noise around you, or when there is heavy rain or strong winds. Also, because the vehicle approach notification device is mounted in front of the vehicle, it may be difficult to hear the rear of the vehicle compared to the front of the vehicle. The alarm may also be heard in the vehicle while the vehicle is in motion, but this is not a malfunction — the sound is intended to inform people around the vehicle that it is approaching.

> **WARNING:** Even if the alarm sounds, you may not notice the approach of the vehicle if the surrounding noise is loud, so drive carefully.

## Acoustic Vehicle Alerting System Warning Light
When the power switch is pressed to change the ignition mode to "ON", this light comes on briefly so you can check that the light is working.

If this light blinks, there may be something wrong with the acoustic vehicle alerting system.
