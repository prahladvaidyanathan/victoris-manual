# Accessory Socket
Your vehicle has accessory sockets in the center console and in the luggage compartment. The sockets operate when the ignition is in ACC or ON mode. Open the rubber or plastic cap on the socket before use.

> **WARNING:** When the accessory socket is not in use, keep the cap closed. Foreign material entering the socket may cause a malfunction or short circuit.

> **NOTICE:**
> - To prevent blowing the fuse, do not exceed a total combined power of 12 volts / 120 watts / 10 amperes across all sockets in use simultaneously.
> - Use only electrical accessories designed for this type of socket.
> - If the accessory socket is in use when the ignition is changed to LOCK (OFF), the audio or navigation system may not turn off, or the keyless entry system may stop working. If this occurs, disconnect the device and check that all electrical equipment is functioning normally. If the problem persists, have the system inspected at a Maruti Suzuki authorized workshop.

> **NOTE:**
> - When stopping the engine, disconnect electrical devices with charging functions such as mobile battery packs. If left connected, the engine may not stop normally.
> - Do not use the accessory socket longer than necessary when the engine is off, to prevent battery discharge.
