# Keyless Push Start System Remote Controller
Your vehicle is equipped with a keyless push start system remote controller. It combines a keyless entry system and a keyless push start system, enabling the following:
- Lock or unlock doors using the LOCK/UNLOCK buttons.
- Lock or unlock doors by pushing the request switch on the door handle or tailgate.
- Start the engine without an ignition key. Refer to [[05-02 Engine Switch Power Switch]] in the "Operating Your Vehicle" section.

> **WARNING:** Radio waves from the keyless push start system antenna(s) may interfere with electrical medical equipment such as pacemakers. Anyone who uses such equipment should consult their medical equipment supplier or medical advisor. If radio wave interference is a concern, have the antenna function disabled by a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.

The remote controller has three buttons:
- **(1) LOCK button**
- **(2) UNLOCK button**
- **(3) Smart power tailgate button**
## Locking and Unlocking with the Remote Controller
To lock or unlock all doors (including the tailgate), operate the remote controller near the vehicle.

**To lock all doors:** Push LOCK button (1) once. The turn signal lights will flash once and the siren will sound once.

**To unlock only the driver's door:** Push UNLOCK button (2) once. The turn signal lights will flash twice and the exterior buzzer will sound twice. If the interior light switch is in the DOOR position, the interior light will turn on for about 15 seconds and then fade out.

**To unlock all other doors:** Push UNLOCK button (2) once again.

> **NOTE:** You can switch the unlock function from requiring two pushes to one push (and vice versa) via the information display setting mode. Refer to [[12-19 Setting Mode of Information Display (Type B)]] in the "Specifications" section.

> **NOTE:**
> - If no door is opened within about 30 seconds after pressing UNLOCK, the doors will automatically lock again.
> - The maximum operating distance is about 5 m, but this can vary near other transmitting devices such as radio towers.
> - The door locks cannot be operated with the remote controller if the ignition mode is other than LOCK (OFF).
> - When any door is open, pressing LOCK will cause the exterior buzzer to sound and the doors cannot be locked.
> - If you lose a remote controller, ask your Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop for a replacement and have the new transmitter code programmed so the old code is erased.
## Locking and Unlocking Using the Request Switches
When the remote controller is within approximately 80 cm of a front door handle or the tailgate switch, you can lock or unlock all doors (including the tailgate) by pushing the request switch (1) on the driver's door handle, front passenger's door handle, or tailgate.

**To lock all doors:** Push one of the request switches once. The turn signal lights will flash once and the siren will sound once.

**To unlock only one door:** Push one of the request switches once.

**To unlock all doors:** Push one of the request switches twice.

> **NOTE:** You can switch from requiring two pushes to one push (and vice versa) via the information display setting mode.

When the doors are unlocked, the turn signal lights will flash twice and the exterior buzzer will sound twice. If the interior light switch is in DOOR position, the interior light will turn on for about 15 seconds.

Check that the doors are locked after using the request switch to lock them.

> **NOTE:**
> - The door locks cannot be operated by the request switch if any door is open or not completely closed, or if the ignition mode is anything other than LOCK (OFF).
> - If no doors are opened within about 30 seconds after unlocking, the doors will lock again automatically.
> - If the remote controller battery is low or there are strong radio waves or noise, the operating range may be reduced or the remote controller may be inoperative.
> - If the remote controller is too close to the door glass, the request switches may not operate.
> - If a spare remote controller is inside the vehicle, the request switches may not operate normally.
> - Each request switch only operates when the remote controller is within that specific switch's range. For example, the driver's door switch will not activate the tailgate switch if the remote controller is only within range of the driver's door.
> - If the remote controller is left in any of the following locations, it may be inadvertently detected and the request switch may activate, risking the remote controller being trapped inside: instrument panel, floor, luggage compartment, glove box, door pockets, or deep storage compartments.

> **NOTICE:** The remote controller is a sensitive electronic instrument. Do not expose it to impacts, moisture, or high temperatures (e.g. direct sunlight on the dashboard). Keep it away from magnetic objects such as televisions.
## Request Switch Warning Buzzer
The exterior buzzer beeps for about 2 seconds to warn you that the request switch is not working in the following conditions:
- The request switch is pressed after all doors are closed with the ignition mode changed to ACC or ON (by pressing the engine/power switch) when the remote controller is outside the vehicle.
- The request switch is pressed after changing the ignition mode to LOCK (OFF) when the remote controller is left inside the vehicle, or any door (including the tailgate) is open.

To resolve: bring the remote controller outside the vehicle if it is inside, and ensure all doors are completely closed, then press the request switch again.
## Reminder Function
If the remote controller is not in the vehicle under the following condition, the buzzer sounds intermittently for about 2 seconds and the immobilizer/keyless push start system warning light on the instrument cluster blinks: when one or more doors are opened and all doors are later closed with the ignition in any other mode than LOCK (OFF).

The indicator light will turn off within several seconds after the remote controller is returned to an area of the vehicle other than the rear luggage area.

If the remote controller is left in the vehicle and you lock the driver's or front passenger's door using the lock knob or the power door locking switch, that door will be automatically unlocked.

> **NOTE:**
> - The reminder will not operate when the remote controller is on the instrument panel, in the glove box, in a storage compartment, in the sun visor, or on the floor.
> - Always carry the remote controller. Do not leave it in the vehicle when leaving.
## Stowing and Removing the Mechanical Key
To stow the key into the remote controller, push the key in until you hear a click.

To remove the key, slide the lock knob (A) in the arrow direction and pull the key out.

Always check that the key is stowed in the remote controller. If the remote controller becomes unusable, you will not be able to lock or unlock the doors.
## General Precautions
> **NOTE:**
> - The keyless push start system may not function correctly near strong signals from a television, power station, or cellular phone; when the remote controller is in contact with or covered by a metal object; when a radio wave type remote keyless entry is used nearby; or when placed near an electronic device such as a personal computer.
> - Always ensure the driver carries the remote controller.
> - If a remote controller is lost, contact your Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop as soon as possible for a replacement and to erase the old code.
> - Up to four remote controllers can be registered for your vehicle.
> - The battery life of the remote controller is approximately two years, but may vary depending on usage.
## Battery Replacement
> **WARNING:** Swallowing a lithium battery may cause serious internal injury or death. Keep lithium batteries away from children and pets. If swallowed, contact a physician immediately.

> **NOTICE:**
> - The remote controller is sensitive. Do not expose it to dust or moisture, and do not tamper with internal parts.
> - When replacing the battery yourself, touch a metallic part to remove static electricity before proceeding to avoid damaging the remote controller.
> - You may damage the remote controller when replacing the battery. Ask your Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop to inspect the system after replacement.

If the remote controller becomes unreliable, replace the battery as follows:

1. Cover a flat-bladed screwdriver with a soft cloth. Insert it into the slot (1) and pry open the remote controller (2). Be careful not to damage the internal unit.
2. Remove the old battery using the flat-bladed screwdriver covered with a soft cloth.
3. Place the new battery (3) — **CR2032 lithium disc type or equivalent** — with its **+ terminal facing up**.
4. Close the remote controller firmly.
5. Verify that the door locks can be operated with the remote controller.
6. Dispose of the used battery properly according to applicable rules and regulations. Do not dispose of lithium batteries with ordinary household trash. The crossed-out wheeled bin symbol on the battery indicates it must be collected separately.

> **NOTE:**
> - Do not dispose of used batteries with ordinary household trash.
> - When purchasing a battery yourself, check the year of manufacture.
> - For more information on disposal or recycling, consult your Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
