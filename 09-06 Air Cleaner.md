# Air Cleaner
If the air cleaner filter element becomes clogged with dust, intake resistance increases, which leads to decreased power output and increased fuel consumption.

## Maintenance Schedule — K15C Engine
| Condition | Clean | Replace |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| Paved road | Every 5,000 km | Every 40,000 km |
| Dusty conditions | Every 2,500 km or as required | Every 40,000 km |

## Service
Since special procedures are required, have air cleaner servicing done at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
