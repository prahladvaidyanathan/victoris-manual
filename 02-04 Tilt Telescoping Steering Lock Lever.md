# Tilt / Telescoping Steering Lock Lever (if equipped)

The lock lever is located under the steering column. To adjust the steering wheel height and fore-aft position:

1. Push down the lock lever to unlock the steering column.
2. Adjust the steering wheel to the desired height and fore-aft position, then lock the steering column by pulling the lock lever up.
3. Try moving the steering wheel up, down, and back and forth to check that it is securely locked in position.

> **WARNING:** Never attempt to adjust the steering wheel while the vehicle is moving. Doing so could cause you to lose control of the vehicle.
