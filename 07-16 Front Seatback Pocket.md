# Front Seatback Pocket
A pocket is provided on the back of each front seat for holding light and soft items such as gloves, newspapers, or magazines.

> **CAUTION:**
> - Do not put hard or breakable objects such as bottles or cans in the pocket. In an accident, these objects could injure the occupants in the rear seat.
> - Do not put sharp or heavy objects in the pocket, as they may damage the seat.
