# USB Socket
The front USB socket (Type A) operates when the ignition is in ACC or ON mode. Use it with a connecting cable to charge your mobile device, or to connect a portable digital music player and enjoy audio through the vehicle's sound system. Refer to the infotainment system manual for audio connection details.

> **NOTICE:** Do not use the USB socket for extended periods when the ignition is in ACC mode or the engine is off, as this may discharge the battery.

> **WARNING:** If any foreign object or liquid enters the USB socket, it may cause a malfunction or short circuit.
