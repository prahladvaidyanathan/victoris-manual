# Luggage Compartment Cover
The luggage compartment cover hides luggage and cargo from view.

> **WARNING:** Do not carry items on top of the luggage compartment cover, even if they are small and light. Objects on top of the cover could be thrown about in an accident, causing injury, or could obstruct the driver's rear view.

> **NOTICE:** To avoid damaging the luggage compartment cover, do not push down on it or lean on it, and handle it carefully when installing or removing.
