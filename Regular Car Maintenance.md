# Daily Car Maintenance
This checklist covers what to inspect on a daily basis, plus periodic checks that should be done weekly and monthly. All checks marked "daily" should be done before every drive.

## Before Every Drive
**Exterior & Visibility**
- Check that all windows, mirrors, lights, and reflectors are clean and unobstructed.
- Visually inspect all four tyres for tread depth, abnormal wear, cracks or damage, loose wheel nuts, and any embedded foreign material (nails, stones, etc.).
- Check under the car for any fluid or oil leakage on the ground.
- Verify that the hood is fully closed and latched.
- Test headlights, turn signal lights, brake lights, and horn for proper operation.

**Cabin Checks**
- Adjust your seat and head restraint before starting.
- Adjust all mirrors (rear-view and side mirrors) to your position.
- Ensure all passengers have properly fastened their seat belts.
- Press the engine start/stop button to switch to ON (without starting the engine) and verify that all warning lights illuminate momentarily — this confirms the bulb check is working.
- Check all gauges (fuel level, temperature, etc.) for normal readings.
- Test the brake pedal — it should feel firm with normal travel. Confirm the Electric Parking Brake (EPB) indicator light turns off when the EPB is released.

## Weekly / Every Fuel Fill
Perform these under-hood checks once a week, every time you fill the tank, or before any long-distance drive.
- **Engine oil level** — check with dipstick; top up with the correct grade if low.
- **Engine coolant level** — check the reservoir; should be between MIN and MAX marks.
- **Brake fluid level** — check reservoir; should be between MIN and MAX marks.
- **Battery electrolyte (acid) level** — check that fluid covers the plates.
- **Windshield washer fluid level** — top up as needed.
- **Hood latch operation** — pull the interior release lever and confirm the hood cannot be lifted further without releasing the secondary latch, then close it securely.

## Monthly / Every Tyre Inspection
- **Tyre pressure** — check all four tyres plus the spare using a tyre pressure gauge. The specified pressures are on the label inside the driver's door frame. Note: the TPMS warning light will alert you if pressure drops significantly, but a manual check is still recommended monthly.
- **Exhaust pipe** — visually check for any holes or cracks. A leaking exhaust can allow carbon monoxide into the cabin.

## Warning Signs — Consult a Maruti Suzuki Authorised Workshop
Do not continue driving if you observe any of the following:
- Oil or other liquid appearing on the ground below the parked vehicle.
- Brake fluid level is low.
- Unusual odors, sounds, or vibrations while driving.
- The steering wheel or brake pedal feel different from normal.
