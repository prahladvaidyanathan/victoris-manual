# Mirrors

> **WARNING:**
> - Always unfold all the outside rearview mirrors and adjust them properly before driving.
> - Do not place objects in the rear of the vehicle which may hinder visibility or interfere with your vision from the rear window glass.
## Inside Rearview Mirror

You can adjust the inside rearview mirror by hand to see the rear of your vehicle. To adjust the mirror, set the selector tab (3) to the day position, then move the mirror up, down, or sideways to obtain the best view. When driving at night, you can move the selector tab (3) to the night position to reduce glare from the headlights of vehicles behind you.

- (1) Night driving
- (2) Day driving
- (3) Selector tab

> **WARNING:**
> - If you adjust the inside rearview mirror while driving, you could lose control of the vehicle, resulting in an accident. Adjust the inside rearview mirror before driving.
> - Only use the night position if it is necessary to reduce glare from the headlights of vehicles behind you. Be aware that in this position you may not be able to see some objects that could be seen in the day position.

> **CAUTION:** Do not modify the inside rearview mirror or install a non-genuine wide mirror. It could result in injury during an accident.
### Auto Dimming Rearview Mirror

You can adjust the auto dimming rearview mirror by hand to see the rear of your vehicle. This mirror automatically reduces glare from the lights of vehicles behind you. The function works when the ignition mode is ON.

When the switch (2) is pushed, the green indicator (1) lights up, confirming the mirror is in automatic dimming mode. To cancel automatic dimming mode, push the switch (2) again; the indicator (1) will go out.

The auto dimming function is automatically deactivated while the gearshift lever is in the "R" position.

> **CAUTION:** The electrolyte may come out if the mirror is broken. Do not allow it to come into contact with your skin or eyes. If it accidentally does, flush with water immediately and consult a doctor.

> **NOTICE:** Do not hook anything heavy on the mirror, as the mirror may break under the weight.

> **NOTE:** The auto dimming system may not work in the following cases:
> - Light from the rear is not directly focused on the sensor (3).
> - The rear window glass is not transparent enough or is dark tinted.
## Outside Rearview Mirrors

Adjust the outside rearview mirrors so you can just see the side of your vehicle in the mirrors.

> **WARNING:** If you adjust the outside rearview mirror while driving, you could lose control of the vehicle, resulting in an accident. Adjust the outside rearview mirrors before driving.
### Electric Mirrors

The switch to control the electric mirrors is located on the driver's door panel. You can adjust the mirrors when the ignition mode is ACC or ON.

1. Move the selector switch (a) to the left or right to select the mirror you wish to adjust.
2. Press the outer part of the switch (a) in the direction you wish to move the mirror.
3. Return the selector switch (a) to the centre position to help prevent unintended adjustment.

> **NOTICE:**
> - The electric outside rearview mirror operates even when the ignition switch is in the ACC position. To prevent unnecessary battery discharge, do not adjust the mirrors longer than necessary while the engine is not running.
> - The mirrors stop moving when they reach the maximum adjusting angle, but the motor continues to operate while the switch is pressed. Do not continue pressing the switch in this situation, as the motor may be damaged.
> - Do not attempt to adjust the outside rearview mirror by hand, as this may cause damage to the motor.
> - Do not fold the electrically foldable outside rearview mirror by hand. It could cause motor failure.
### Outside Rearview Mirror Folding Switch

You can fold the mirrors when parking in a narrow space. Push the folding switch to fold and unfold the mirrors. Make sure the mirrors are completely unfolded before you start driving.

> **WARNING:** Do not drive the vehicle with the outside rearview mirrors folded. Doing so may result in an accident.

> **CAUTION:** Moving mirrors can pinch and injure a hand. Do not allow anyone's hand to get near the mirrors when folding and unfolding them.

> **NOTE:**
> - If you press the folding switch to open the mirrors and simultaneously start the engine, the mirrors may get stuck momentarily. If this happens, press the folding switch again after starting the engine.
> - A slight time difference in the opening of both mirrors is normal and is due to the angular positioning of the driver's seat.
