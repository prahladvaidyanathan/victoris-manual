# eCall
The eCall system automatically sends vehicle information to an emergency contact centre and connects you to an eCall operator whenever an airbag deploys, or when the SOS button is pressed manually.

## How It Works
1. The vehicle's location is gathered by the eCall system.
2. Vehicle information is transmitted to the emergency contact centre.
3. The emergency contact centre informs the regional emergency service provider.
4. The regional emergency service provider attempts to contact the vehicle occupant to assess the situation and assistance required.

> **WARNING:** Radio waves from the eCall system may interfere with electrical medical equipment such as pacemakers. Anyone who uses such equipment should consult their medical equipment supplier or medical advisor about whether radio waves from the eCall system may interfere with it.

> **CAUTION:**
> - eCall will not function without a valid telematics contract.
> - eCall is only available where wireless mobile communication is possible. It cannot be used in areas with atmospheric disturbance, remote areas, or areas where a signal cannot be obtained.
> - If the microphone, speaker, or SOS button is malfunctioning, eCall cannot operate properly. Have the function checked at a Maruti Suzuki authorized workshop.
> - Use a Maruti Suzuki genuine audio system for optimal eCall operation.

> **NOTE:**
> - The eCall system's correct functioning depends on the presence of an eCall operator in the country and the availability of its services.
> - The responsibility of components of the eCall system (other than equipment installed on the vehicle) is borne by the operator of the eCall system.
> - eCall may not work in the following situations — in such cases, contact emergency services by other means (e.g. a nearby public phone):
>   - Poor reception or a busy line, even within the cellular service area.
>   - When the vehicle is outside the cellular service area.
>   - When any related equipment (SOS button panel, indicator lights, microphone, DCM, antenna, or connecting wires) is malfunctioning, damaged, or broken.

## Automatic Operation Mode
The eCall system activates when the ignition is switched to ON. If an airbag deploys in an accident, vehicle information (position, direction, VIN, accident time, etc.) is sent automatically to the emergency contact centre. The green indicator will blink and the call will connect to an eCall operator.

## Manual Operation Mode
Open the cover (5), then press and hold the SOS button (1) for more than 1 second and release it. The green indicator will blink and the call will connect to an eCall operator.

> **NOTE:** Do not perform a test operation using manual mode. If the system is accidentally activated, cancel it immediately — refer to "Cancellation of eCall" below.

## During a Call (Both Modes)
- A tone will sound when the cancellation period ends and the emergency call connects.
- Vehicle information (location, direction, VIN, accident time, etc.) is sent to the emergency contact centre only when eCall operates — not at other times.
- Conversation during the call is not recorded by the vehicle, but is recorded by the emergency contact centre.
- Communication from the eCall operator is heard through the speaker on the centre of the instrument panel. If the audio system is in use, it is automatically muted and eCall is prioritised.
- The eCall system has a built-in backup battery for use when the main vehicle battery is damaged in an accident. Its useful life is approximately 3 years under temperatures between −40 °C and 85 °C.
- If the built-in battery was used during an automatic eCall operation, have it replaced at a Maruti Suzuki authorized workshop.
- When installing electrical equipment such as a navigation terminal, consult a Maruti Suzuki authorized workshop to avoid interfering with eCall components.

## Indicator Lights
| Green indicator | Red indicator | State |
|----------------|---------------|-------|
| ON | ON | For approximately 5–90 seconds after ignition is switched to ON |
| ON | OFF | Normal operation (including callback waiting) |
| Blinks ~every 0.5 s | OFF | eCall operation begins |
| Blinks ~every 1 s | OFF | eCall communication established — voice call or callback |
| Blinks ~every 0.3 s | OFF | eCall communication finished normally |
| OFF | Blinks ~every 0.3 s | eCall communication finished abnormally |
| Blinks ~every 3 s | OFF | No GPS satellite connection, or out of modem network range |
| OFF | Blinks ~every 1 s | System error, or built-in battery needs replacement |
| OFF | OFF | No eCall contract |

> **NOTE:** It may take up to 90 seconds for the eCall system to become available after the ignition is switched to ON. If the SOS button is pressed while both indicators are ON, eCall will begin once the system is ready.

## Warning Messages
If "SERVICE CONNECTED" or "Service Connected" appears on the multi-information display, eCall is in progress and other connected services are temporarily unavailable.

If "EMERGENCY CALL IN PROGRESS" or "Emergency Call in Progress" appears, along with the red indicator blinking every 1 second, the built-in battery may be undercharged or there is a system error. Have the vehicle inspected at a Maruti Suzuki authorized workshop promptly. Replace the battery as soon as possible — remaining charge may only support approximately 10 minutes of communication.

## Cancellation of eCall
eCall can only be cancelled in manual operation mode, and only before communication is established with an operator. Press the SOS button again within 5 seconds of the initial press to cancel.

> **NOTE:**
> - Communication cannot be cancelled in automatic operation mode.
> - Once communication is established with an eCall operator, it cannot be cancelled from the vehicle — ask the emergency contact centre to cancel.

## Battery Disposal
> **WARNING:** Improper handling of the built-in lithium battery in the eCall unit may result in heat generation, explosion, or fire. Do not heat, disassemble, deform, or dispose of the battery in fire. Used batteries must be disposed of separately from ordinary household waste in accordance with applicable regulations. Consult a Maruti Suzuki authorized workshop for battery replacement or disposal.
