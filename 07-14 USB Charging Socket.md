# USB Charging Socket
Two rear USB Type-C charging sockets are provided for charging mobile devices. They operate when the ignition is in ACC or ON mode.

The total maximum combined output of both sockets is 60 W (USB Power Delivery 3.1). Output is distributed as follows:
- **First connected socket:** Up to 15 V / 3.0 A (45 W), automatically adjusted based on the connected device.
- **Second connected socket:** 5 V / 3.0 A (15 W).

> **NOTICE:** Do not use the USB charging sockets for extended periods when the ignition is in ACC mode or the engine is off, as this may discharge the battery.

> **WARNING:** If any foreign object or liquid enters a USB socket, it may cause a malfunction or short circuit.
