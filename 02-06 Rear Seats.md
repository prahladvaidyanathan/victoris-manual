# Rear Seats
## Head Restraints

Head restraints are designed to help reduce the risk of neck injuries in the case of an accident.

To raise a rear head restraint, pull it upward until it clicks. To lower it, push down the head restraint while holding in the lock button (1). To remove it entirely (for cleaning, replacement, etc.), push in the lock button (1) and pull the head restraint all the way out.

> **NOTE:** It may be necessary to fold the seatback forward to provide enough overhead clearance to remove the head restraint.

> **WARNING:**
> - Never drive with the head restraints removed. Without a head restraint, a passenger's backward tilting cannot be prevented in a collision, and the impact to the head cannot be reduced during sudden braking or a collision. This may cause severe injury.
> - If the head restraint is installed back to front or not installed securely, it may not provide best performance in an emergency, resulting in severe injury. If installed back to front, you cannot adjust the height or install it securely. Always install the head restraint securely in the correct direction.
> - Do not attempt to adjust the head restraint while driving. Being distracted by head restraint adjustment could lead to an accident.
> - If you are installing a child restraint system and it touches the head restraint, a child may be severely injured in a collision. When installing a child restraint system, adjust the head restraint to its highest position or remove it to prevent contact with the child restraint system.

> **CAUTION:** If you leave a removed head restraint in the vehicle, it may jump out and hit passengers or objects, resulting in injury or damage. Do not leave a removed head restraint in the vehicle.
## Rear Centre Head Restraint

When using the rear centre head restraint, pull it up and lock it securely before driving.

> **WARNING:** Do not allow anyone to sit in the rear centre seat with the rear centre head restraint removed or in the stowed position.
