# Rearview Camera (if equipped)
When the gearshift lever is shifted to "R" while the ignition mode is ON, the rearview camera automatically displays the view behind the vehicle on the infotainment screen. The camera is installed on the tailgate.

When the gearshift lever is moved out of "R", the display returns to whatever was showing previously. The rearview camera display has first priority over any other display mode, but will not show the rear view while the system is initialising.

> **WARNING:** The distance shown on the rearview camera display may differ from the actual distance depending on road conditions or vehicle loading. The camera's display area is also limited. Reversing while looking only at the display may cause an accident. The rearview camera is a driving aid only — the driver is solely responsible for parking and similar manoeuvres. Always confirm safety directly with your eyes and mirrors, and check that the tailgate is securely closed before reversing.

> **NOTICE:** If the rearview camera is used for an extended period with the ignition ON but the engine not running, the battery may discharge. Do not leave the ignition ON for a long time with the engine off.

## Display Range
The rearview camera shows the area behind the rear bumper. It cannot show:
- Objects that are very close to or directly under the bumper
- Obstacles higher than the camera (upper portions of tall objects such as road signs will not appear)

Images on the display are mirror images (reversed left-to-right).

## Guide Lines
The following guide lines are overlaid on the camera display:

| Guide line | Colour | Distance from rear bumper centre |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle width lines (vertical) | Purple | Slightly outside the vehicle width, including mirrors |
| Distance line | Light blue | Approximately 2 m |
| Distance line | Yellow | Approximately 1 m |
| Distance line | Red | Approximately 0.5 m |

> **NOTE:** Guide lines are projected onto the road surface plane, so they do not accurately represent the distance to obstacles that are raised off the ground (such as a parked vehicle). They are rough indications only. If the suspension has been modified or non-standard tyres fitted, the guide lines will not be accurate.

## Distance Accuracy on Slopes
The rearview camera display may misrepresent distance when there is a slope behind the vehicle:

**Uphill behind the vehicle:** Objects appear farther away than they actually are.

**Downhill behind the vehicle:** Objects appear closer than they actually are.

## Camera Care
> **NOTICE:** The rearview camera is a precision instrument.
> - Do not strike the camera.
> - Do not remove snow or mud from the lens using a stick.
> - Do not use a high-pressure washer around the camera — water ingress may cause malfunction or fire.
> - Do not use a brush to clean the lens.
> - Do not use alcohol, benzene, or thinner on the lens.
> - Do not apply wax to the camera lens.
> - The lens is hard-coated — damage or discolouration may obscure the image.

If body wax gets on the lens, wipe it off with a clean cloth dampened with mild detergent diluted with water, then wipe dry.

## Display May Be Difficult to See In
The following conditions may make the image harder to see, but are not system malfunctions:
- Dark areas, rain, or night-time
- Extreme temperatures around the lens, or wet conditions causing lens condensation
- Mud, dust, or water droplets on or around the lens
- Strong light shining directly into the camera (may cause vertical lines on display)
- Fluorescent lighting environments (display may flicker)
- Low outside temperatures (image may appear darkened)

## Troubleshooting
**If the rear view is not shown:**
- Check that the ignition mode is ON
- Check that the gearshift lever is in "R"

**If the image is poor:**
- Check that the camera lens is clean
- Check that sunlight or another vehicle's headlights are not shining directly into the lens

If the system is still not working properly after checking the above, have it inspected by a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop as soon as possible.
