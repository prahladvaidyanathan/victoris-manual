# Drink Holder
Drink holders are provided at the following locations:

- **Floor console:** Between the front seats.
- **Rear seat armrest:** In the center of the rear seatback. Tilt the armrest down and forward to use.
- **Door:** Bottle holders are located in each door.

> **WARNING:**
> - Use a lid for drinks to prevent spilling. Spilling hot liquid can cause burns. Spilling liquid on the navigation system, audio system, or associated switches; on the wiring or electrical components under the floor; on the moving parts of the gearshift lever or seatbelt buckle; or on the lithium-ion battery located under the front left seat (on SHVS models) may cause a fire, electric shock, or airbag system failure.
> - Do not place long containers, sharp-edged, hard, or breakable objects in the drink holders — they may be thrown out during driving and cause personal injury or obstruct your view.
> - Do not place a long drink container in the driver's side drink holder, as it may obstruct your view and cause an accident.

> **NOTICE:** Do not lean on the rear seat armrest or allow a child to sit on it, as this may cause damage. After fastening the seat belt, always check that the armrest is not interfering with the belt.

> **NOTE:**
> - Maruti Suzuki recommends a maximum bottle capacity of 1 litre.
> - Only place bottles with a cap in the holders.
> - Do not place the keyless remote controller in a drink or bottle holder, as it may not be detected.
> - If liquid is spilled or dropped accidentally, have the vehicle inspected at a Maruti Suzuki authorized workshop.
