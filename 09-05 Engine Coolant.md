# Engine Coolant
## Specified Coolant — K15C Engine
**Coolant colour: Blue**

Use Maruti Suzuki genuine coolant or equivalent. This coolant maintains proper engine temperature and protects against freezing, boiling, corrosion, and rust.

> [!notice] To avoid damaging your cooling system:
> - Mix ratio must be **50/50 coolant to distilled water**. Do not exceed 50/50 concentration — higher concentrations cause overheating.
> - Do not use straight coolant or plain water.
> - Do not add extra inhibitors or additives — they may be incompatible with the cooling system.
> - Do not mix different types of base coolants — this can accelerate seal wear and cause severe overheating or transmission damage.
> - The coolant supplied is already pre-diluted. Do **not** dilute further with distilled water.

## Coolant Level Check
Check the coolant level at the **reservoir tank**, not at the radiator. Do this with the engine cool.

The level should be between the **FULL** and **LOW** marks on the reservoir tank.

## Adding Coolant
> [!warning] Swallowing engine coolant can cause severe injury or death. Inhaling coolant mist or vapours or getting coolant in your eyes could result in severe injury.
> - Do not drink antifreeze or coolant solution. If swallowed, do not induce vomiting — contact a poison control centre or a physician immediately.
> - Avoid inhaling coolant mist or hot vapours. If inhaled, move to fresh air.
> - If coolant gets in the eyes, flush with water and seek medical attention.
> - Wash thoroughly after handling coolant.
> - Keep coolant out of reach of children and pets.

If the coolant level is below the **LOW** mark:

1. Ensure the engine is cool before proceeding.
2. Remove the reservoir tank cap, aligning the marks on the cap and tank.
3. Add coolant until the level reaches the **FULL** mark.
4. Never fill above the **FULL** mark.
5. When replacing the cap, line up the mark on the cap with the mark on the tank to avoid coolant leakage.

## Coolant Replacement
Since special procedures are required, have coolant replacement done at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
