# Parking Sensors
The parking sensor system uses ultrasonic sensors mounted in the rear bumper to detect obstacles. The sensors transmit ultrasonic waves, and the time taken for the signal to reflect back from an obstacle is used to calculate its distance.

The Victoris ZXi Petrol 6AT is equipped with **rear sensors only** (RPAS): 2 rear center sensors and 2 rear corner sensors.

> **WARNING:** The parking sensor warns you of obstacles using buzzers and by showing obstacle positions on the information display. However, you must still pay full attention while driving. The sensors detect obstacles only within a limited area and speed range. In tricky areas, move the vehicle slowly while checking your surroundings using direct vision and mirrors. Over-reliance on parking sensors increases the risk of an accident.

> **NOTICE:** Parking sensors are for driver assistance only.

## Sensor Operation
The rear sensors activate based on gearshift lever position:

| Gearshift position | Rear center sensors | Rear corner sensors |
|---|---|---|
| R | On | On |
| N or D | Off | Off |

## Detection Range
The sensors can detect obstacles such as a wall up to approximately **150 cm (5 ft)** from the rear of the vehicle. Obstacles within approximately 20 cm of a sensor or directly below a sensor are not detectable.

## Obstacle Indication
When an obstacle is detected, an interior buzzer sounds and a symbol representing the obstacle's position appears on the information display. The symbol and buzzer pattern change with distance:

| Distance (approx.) | Buzzer | Display |
|---|---|---|
| 60–150 cm | Short beeps at long intervals | Three lines |
| 45–60 cm | Short beeps at short intervals | Three lines |
| 35–45 cm | Short beeps at very short intervals | Two lines |
| Less than 35 cm | Continuous beep | One line |

The corner sensors activate within the 45–60 cm range and below, alongside the center sensors.

Additional notes:
- Symbols appear with a short delay after obstacle detection.
- If multiple obstacles are detected simultaneously, all positions are shown on the display, but the buzzer sounds only for the nearest one.
- The parking sensor indicator light blinks when an obstacle is detected while the system is operating.

## Parking Sensor Buzzer OFF Switch
If you press the parking sensor buzzer OFF switch while the buzzer is sounding, the buzzer is temporarily silenced. The buzzer will resume in any of the following situations:
- The parking sensor buzzer OFF switch is pressed again
- The gearshift lever is moved from a non-reverse position to R
- The gearshift lever is moved from a non-drive position to D/M/N

> **NOTE:** The buzzer may sound again if an obstacle is detected while the buzzer is paused.

## Sensor Precautions
> **WARNING:** Observe the following to prevent sensor malfunction. Failure to do so may result in the sensor not operating properly, possibly leading to an accident.

- Keep sensors clean at all times. Wipe clean with a soft cloth if dirty, wet, or covered with snow.
- Do not attach accessories, stickers, or other items to the sensors or the surrounding area.
- Do not subject the sensors or surrounding area to impact. If the rear bumper sustains an impact, have the sensors inspected by a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
- Do not change the position or orientation of the sensors, or remove them.
- Do not disassemble the sensors.
- Do not modify or paint the sensors, or replace them with non-genuine parts.
- Do not point a high-pressure washer nozzle at the sensors.
- Do not direct steam cleaning too close to the sensors.
- If the sensors are removed and reinstalled, or if the rear bumper is replaced, angle measurement and initialisation of the sensors will be necessary — consult a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.

## Situations Where the Sensor May Not Activate Properly
- When dirt, snow, or ice is attached to the sensor (remove the foreign object)
- In bad weather such as heavy rain, fog, or a blizzard
- When backward visibility is poor due to exhaust gas, water vapour, steam, sand, or smoke
- When the sensor is covered by a sticker or accessory
- When commercially available accessories are installed on the bumper (tow hooks, fog lamps, corner poles, radio antenna, etc.)
- When the vehicle height or bumper position has been altered (e.g. suspension modification)
- When sensor alignment is disturbed due to a collision
- When snow chains or non-standard tyre sizes are fitted
- When the vehicle is on a slope, rough road, gravel, or long grass
- When the vehicle is significantly inclined
- When there is ultrasonic interference from other sources (another vehicle's horn, motorcycle engine, air brakes from a large vehicle, another parking sensor system)
- When the sensor is too close to an obstacle
- When the vehicle approaches an obstacle at a diagonal angle or toward the edge or corner of a wall
- When an obstacle is not vertical (angled walls, corrugated or wavy shutters)
- When an obstacle protrudes from a wall (pipes, poles)

The following types of obstacles may not be detected reliably:
- Sound-absorbing objects (people, sponge-like materials, snow banks)
- Moving objects (pedestrians, bicycles, animals)
- Objects that rush into the vehicle's path suddenly
- Thin pole-like objects (signs, street lamps)
- Low-profile objects (guardrails, kerbs)
- Small or thin objects (chains, ropes, wire mesh fences)

> **NOTE:** Thin poles or obstacles lower than the sensors may become undetectable as the vehicle moves closer, even if detected from a greater distance. The system may calculate the distance to a road sign as shorter than the actual distance.

## Temporary Stop and Failure
The parking sensors stop temporarily or permanently in the following conditions:

**Temporary stop** (resumes when condition improves):
- Foreign object (dirt, snow, ice) attached to a sensor
- Battery voltage abnormality

**Sensor failure** (system stops until engine is restarted):
- Parking sensor indicator light comes on
- Master warning indicator blinks

If the parking sensor indicator light remains on after restarting the engine, there may be a sensor failure. Have the vehicle inspected by a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop. The vehicle can still be driven normally while the system is stopped.

## Warning and Indicator Messages
**"Clean Parking Sensors"** — A sensor is contaminated. Wipe it clean with a soft cloth. A blinking line appears in the affected sensor's position on the display.

**"Service Parking Sensors"** — There may be a problem with the parking sensor system. Have the vehicle inspected by a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.

## Situations Where the System Does Not Activate
- For a few seconds immediately after changing the ignition mode to ON
- When the gearshift lever position does not match the selector position shown in the information display, or when "–" is displayed
- When an obstacle cannot be detected
- When the system is temporarily stopped or malfunctioning
