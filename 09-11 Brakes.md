**Brake fluid:** Replace every 40,000 km or 24 months, whichever comes first.

## Brake Fluid
Check the brake fluid level by looking at the reservoir in the engine compartment. The level should be between the **MAX** and **MIN** marks.

**Specified fluid: Brake Fluid DOT3**. Do not use reclaimed fluid or fluid stored in old or open containers. Keep foreign particles and other liquids out of the reservoir.

> [!warning] Failure to follow these guidelines can result in personal injury or serious brake system trouble.
> - If the brake warning light comes on (with engine running and parking brake fully disengaged), immediately ask a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop to inspect the brake system.
> - A rapid fluid loss indicates leakage in the brake system — get it inspected immediately.
> - Brake fluid is harmful or fatal if swallowed or if it comes in contact with skin or eyes. If swallowed, do not induce vomiting — contact a poison control centre or physician immediately. If it gets in eyes, flush with water and seek medical attention. Wash thoroughly after handling. Keep out of reach of children and animals.

> [!notice]
> - If the brake fluid level is near the MIN line, there is a possibility of brake pad/shoe wear or fluid leakage. Ask a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop to inspect the brake system.
> - Brake fluid can damage painted surfaces — be careful when refilling the reservoir.
> - Brake fluid absorbs water over time. Fluid with high water content can cause vapour lock or brake system malfunction. Replace per the maintenance schedule.

## Brake Pedal
**Minimum distance between brake pedal and floor carpet "a": 52 mm (K15C)**

To check: with the engine running, press the pedal with approximately 30 kg of force and measure the distance between the pedal and the floor carpet. Do not include floor mat or rubber in the measurement. Measure at the side of the pedal facing the driver's door.

The brake system is self-adjusting — no pedal adjustment is needed. If the distance is less than the minimum, have the vehicle inspected at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.

> [!warning] If the brake pad lining has worn to minimum thickness, a squealing noise will sound during braking. Have the vehicle inspected immediately at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop. Continued driving with this condition can cause an accident.

> [!caution] Have the vehicle inspected immediately if you notice any of the following:
> - Poor braking performance
> - Uneven braking (brakes not working uniformly on all wheels)
> - Excessive pedal travel
> - Brake dragging

## Parking Brake Lever (if equipped)
Check for proper adjustment by counting the ratchet clicks as you slowly pull the lever to full engagement.

| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| Ratchet tooth count "b" | 4th – 9th click |
| Lever pull force | 200 N (20 kg) |

If the brake is not properly adjusted, or if brake drag is suspected after the lever is fully released, have the parking brake inspected and/or adjusted at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.

## Brake Discs
| Location | Minimum thickness "a" |
|----------|----------------------|
| Front wheels | 20.0 mm |
| Rear wheels | 8.0 mm |

If measured thickness falls below or exceeds these values, replace the parts. Measurement requires disassembly using a micrometer or caliper — refer to the relevant service manual or have it done at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
