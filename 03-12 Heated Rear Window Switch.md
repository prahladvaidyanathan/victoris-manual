# Heated Rear Window Switch
While the engine is running, you can use the heated rear window switch as a defogger for the rear window glass.

Push the rear defogger switch to heat the rear window glass — the indicator light (1) will turn on. Push the switch again to turn the defogger off.

The defogger will automatically turn off after 15 minutes to prevent battery drain.

If you change the ignition mode to ACC or LOCK (OFF) with the defogger on, the defogger will not automatically resume when you start the engine again.

> **NOTICE:**
> - The heated rear window uses a large amount of electricity. Turn off the switch shortly after the window has become clear to protect the battery. Do not use the defogger to melt snow or dry rain water.
> - The defogger will only work when the engine is running.

> **NOTE:** When wiping the interior side of the rear window glass, use a soft cloth dampened with water and wipe along the heat wires to avoid damaging the wires and terminals.
