# Automatic Transmission Fluid (if equipped)
## Specified Fluid
| Transmission type | Fluid |
|------------------|-------|
| 6AT model | SUZUKI AT OIL AW-1 |
| e-CVT model | AUTO FLUID WS |

## Maintenance Schedule — K15C Engine
| Item | 40k km | 60k km | 80k km |
|------|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Automatic transaxle fluid (leakage) | I | I | I |

## Service
Fluid level check, fluid change, and all related procedures require special tools and materials. Have all automatic transmission fluid work done at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
