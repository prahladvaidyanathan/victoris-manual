# Fuel Filler Opening
The fuel filler door is located on the left rear side of the vehicle. Pull up the opener lever located on the outboard lower side of the driver's seat to unlock it. The door locks automatically when closed.

> **WARNING:** Observe the following points when refueling to prevent fire:
> - Always stop the engine first.
> - Close all doors and windows when refueling.
> - As gasoline is highly flammable, open sparks such as lit cigarettes are strictly prohibited.
## Opening and Closing the Fuel Filler Cap
Turn the cap counter-clockwise to open. Turn it clockwise to close until you hear more than 2 clicks.

> **NOTE:** When refueling, the cap holder hooks onto the groove of the fuel filler cap to keep it secure while you work.
