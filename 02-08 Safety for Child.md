# Safety for Child

For safety, carefully drive at lower speed than usual when children are in the vehicle.
## Place a Child in the Rear Seat

- Seat a child next to an adult when possible, and ensure that the child can be visually monitored.
- Select an appropriate child restraint system suitable for the child's age and physical size.

> **WARNING:**
> - If you do not use the child-proof lock, a child may open a door and be thrown out, resulting in severe injury. Use the child-proof lock when children are in the vehicle.
> - When it is necessary to seat a child who does not need a child restraint system in the front passenger's seat, slide the front passenger's seat all the way back. If the front passenger's seat is in a forward position, there is a risk of serious injury due to major impact if the front passenger's seat airbag deploys.
> - If a child puts a hand out of the window or leans on a door, the child may be severely injured by the impact of side or curtain airbag activation. Do not allow a child to put a hand out of the window or lean on a door.
> - Do not allow the hands or feet of a child near movable parts inside the vehicle, such as armrests and seat rails. Otherwise, there is a possibility of injury.
## A Child Needs to Wear Seat Belt

> **WARNING:**
> - Do not drive with a child on your lap. Even if you hold the child tightly, proper support is not possible, and in the event of a collision the child may be seriously injured.
> - If a child does not wear a seat belt, in a sudden braking or a collision the child may be seriously injured. Always have a child wear a seat belt.
> - Do not allow two or more people to use a single seat belt. In a sudden braking or collision, the seat belt may not provide best performance, resulting in severe injury.
> - The seat belts of this vehicle are designed for occupants of adult size. If the shoulder strap rests on the neck or chin of a child rather than the shoulder, or if the lap portion of the belt does not rest securely on the hipbone, ensure that the child is seated in a child restraint system placed in the rear seat. Failure to properly use a seat belt may result in serious injury to the child.
> - Use a child restraint system securely fastened to the rear seat for any child that cannot hold their head upright or sit on their own.
## Do not Let a Child Play with Seat Belt

> **WARNING:** If a child plays with a seat belt and the child's body becomes wrapped by the belt, it may cause severe injury such as suffocation. In case of emergency, cut the seat belt with scissors.
## Using the Child Restraint System

Refer to [[02-13 Child Restraint Systems]] for instructions on securing a child restraint system using the seat belt.
## An Adult Needs to Open or Close Each Door, Window, Tailgate, and Adjust Seats

Only an adult should open or close each door or window and adjust seats, to prevent a child's hands, arms, legs, or neck from getting caught.

> **WARNING:**
> - If the windows are not locked, a child may operate them by mistake, or other passengers may get caught in the windows. Use the window lock switch to prevent unintended operation of the power windows by a child.
> - If you leave the power window in a ready condition, a child may cause unintended operation, resulting in an accident. When leaving the vehicle, press the engine switch to change the ignition mode to OFF and keep the key with you.
## Do not Let a Child Stick Head or Hands out of the Window

> **WARNING:** Ensure that a child does not put their hands or head out of the windows. Serious injuries due to falling out or sudden braking may occur. A child could also be seriously injured by coming into contact with objects outside the vehicle.
## Do not Leave a Child Unattended in the Vehicle

> **WARNING:**
> - When getting out of the vehicle, do not allow a child to stay in the vehicle without you. Unintended operations by a child may cause a fire or an accident. The inside temperature of the vehicle will become hot under the blazing sun; a child may suffer from heat stroke or dehydration, resulting in severe health problems or death in the worst case. Even if you turn on the air conditioner, do not leave a child alone in the vehicle.
> - Never leave children unattended in the vehicle and never allow children to have or use the key. Children may be able to start the vehicle or shift it into neutral. There is also a danger that children may injure themselves by playing with the windows or other features of the vehicle.
## Do not Place a Child in the Luggage Compartment

> **WARNING:**
> - The luggage area does not feature a structure that can accommodate people. Do not allow a child to ride in this area. Otherwise, there is a danger of accidents due to sudden braking. Do not let a child play in the luggage area even while the vehicle is stopped.
> - Do not let small children operate equipment which may catch or pinch body parts, such as the power window, hood, tailgate, or seats. Do not allow children to play in the luggage compartment. If a child is accidentally locked in the luggage compartment, they could suffer heat exhaustion or other injuries.
