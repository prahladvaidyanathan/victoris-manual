# Sun Visor
The sun visors can be pulled down to block glare coming through the windshield, or unhooked and turned to the side to block glare coming through the side window.

> **NOTICE:** When unhooking or rehooking a sun visor, handle it by the hard plastic parts only, or it may be damaged.
## Card Holder
A card holder is provided on the back of the sun visor. Slide your card into the holder to secure it while driving.

> **NOTICE:** If you park your vehicle outdoors in direct sunlight in hot weather, the interior heat may distort or crack plastic cards. Do not leave cards in the card holder under these conditions.
## Vanity Mirror
To use the vanity mirror on the back of the sun visor, pull up the mirror cover.

> **WARNING:**
> - Do not use the vanity mirror while driving, as it may cause you to lose control of the vehicle and result in an accident.
> - Do not place any object near an airbag storage location when using the vanity mirror. Doing so may cause the airbag to deploy, causing serious injury.
