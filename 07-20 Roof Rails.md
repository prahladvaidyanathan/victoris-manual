# Roof Rails
The roof rails can be used to attach an optional roof rack, available from a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop. If using a roof rack, follow the instructions provided with it in addition to the precautions below.
## Loading on a Roof Rack
- Check that the roof rack is securely installed before use.
- To mount cargo such as skis or bicycles, use suitable attachments available from a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop. Do not mount cargo directly on the roof panel.
- The maximum permissible gross weight of the roof rack plus cargo is **75 kg**. Do not exceed this limit, and ensure the gross vehicle weight does not exceed the GVWR listed in the Specifications section.
- Mount the heaviest items at the bottom and distribute cargo as evenly as possible.
- Secure the front and rear ends of long items such as wood panels and surfboards to both the front and rear of the vehicle. Protect painted surfaces from scuffing caused by tie-down ropes.
- Do not carry large items that hang over the bumpers or the sides of the vehicle, as they may block your view.
- Check periodically that cargo is securely fastened and that the roof rack is free from damage.

> **WARNING:**
> - Abrupt manoeuvres or improperly secured cargo can allow cargo to fly off the vehicle and hit others, causing personal injury or property damage. Avoid jackrabbit starts, sharp turns, fast cornering, and sudden braking when carrying roof cargo.
> - Large, bulky, long, or flat items can affect vehicle aerodynamics or be caught by the wind, reducing vehicle control and risking an accident. Drive cautiously at a reduced speed when carrying this type of cargo.
