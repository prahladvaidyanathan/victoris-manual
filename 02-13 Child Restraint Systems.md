# Child Restraint Systems

> **WARNING:**
> - Always secure children in an appropriate child restraint system. Children who are not properly restrained can be severely injured in a collision or sudden braking.
> - Infants and small children should always be seated in the rear seats. If an infant or small child must be placed in the front passenger's seat, move the seat as far back as possible and ensure the child is properly secured in a child restraint system. A child restraint system used in the front seat could be struck by a deploying airbag, causing serious injury or death.
> - Never put a rear-facing child restraint system in the front passenger's seat. A deploying airbag can strike it with great force, causing serious or fatal injury to the child.
> - Always follow the child restraint system manufacturer's instructions when installing and using a child restraint system.
> - Never hold a child in your arms while in a moving vehicle. In a collision, the child can be torn from your arms and seriously injured.
> - Do not allow children to ride in the luggage area. In a collision or sudden stop, they may be seriously injured.
## Types of Child Restraint Systems

Child restraint systems are categorised by the age and size of the child they are designed to protect:

**Infant seat:** Designed for infants from birth. Infant seats are always installed facing the rear of the vehicle. Infants should remain in a rear-facing seat as long as possible, until they reach the maximum weight or height allowed by the seat manufacturer.

**Child seat:** Designed for children who have outgrown their infant seat. Child seats may be installed facing the front or rear of the vehicle, depending on the child's weight and the seat manufacturer's instructions.

**Booster seat:** Designed for children who have outgrown their forward-facing child seat but are not yet large enough to use an adult seat belt safely. A booster seat raises the child so that the seat belt fits properly.

## Securing a Child Restraint System by Seat Belt

1. Place the child restraint system on the rear seat, following the manufacturer's instructions.
2. If the head restraint interferes with the child restraint system, adjust the head restraint to its highest position or remove it.
3. Route the seat belt through or around the child restraint system as instructed by its manufacturer.
4. Insert the latch plate into the buckle until you hear a click.
5. Remove any slack from the belt by pulling the shoulder portion upward while pressing down on the child restraint system.
6. Pull the lap portion of the belt to confirm there is no slack.
7. Push and pull the child restraint system firmly in various directions to confirm it is securely installed.

> **WARNING:**
> - Install the child restraint system on a rear seat whenever possible. Placing it on the front passenger's seat is dangerous because a deploying airbag can strike the child restraint system.
> - Never use a single belt to secure more than one child restraint system or more than one occupant.
> - After installing the child restraint system, always check that it is firmly secured. An improperly secured child restraint system can cause serious injury in a collision.

> **CAUTION:** Make sure the seat belt is not twisted when securing the child restraint system.
## Securing a Child Restraint System by ISOFIX

ISOFIX lower anchorages are located at the rear outboard seating positions. The anchorages are marked with the ISOFIX symbol.

To locate the lower anchorages, press down on the seatback-to-cushion junction between the seatback and the seat cushion. The lower anchorage bars can be felt through the upholstery.

**Attaching the ISOFIX connectors:**
1. Position the child restraint system on the seat.
2. If the head restraint interferes with the child restraint system, adjust it to its highest position or remove it.
3. Push the child restraint system firmly rearward into the seat.
4. Attach the ISOFIX connectors to the lower anchorage bars until you hear a click.
5. Pull the child restraint system forward to confirm the connectors are securely latched.
6. Push and pull the child restraint system firmly in various directions to confirm it is securely installed.

> **WARNING:**
> - Make sure the ISOFIX connectors are securely latched to the lower anchorage bars. An improperly latched child restraint system may not protect the child in a collision.
> - Always use the top tether when required by the child restraint system manufacturer's instructions.
## Installation with Top Tether

A top tether anchorage is provided on the rear shelf area for use with forward-facing ISOFIX child restraint systems that have a top tether strap.

To attach the top tether:
1. Route the top tether strap over the rear seatback and toward the rear shelf.
2. Locate the top tether anchorage hook point on the rear shelf.
3. Attach the top tether hook to the anchorage.
4. Tighten the top tether strap according to the child restraint system manufacturer's instructions.
5. Confirm the strap is taut and the child restraint system does not move excessively.

> **WARNING:** Do not attach the top tether to the luggage restraint loops. These loops are designed only for securing luggage, not for child restraint systems. Using them as a top tether anchorage will not adequately restrain the child restraint system in a collision and may cause serious injury.
