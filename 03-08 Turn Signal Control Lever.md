# Turn Signal Control Lever
The turn signal control lever can be used when the ignition mode is ON.
## Turn Signal Operation
Move the lever (1) upward to indicate a left turn, or downward to indicate a right turn. The turn signal lights and their indicators on the instrument cluster will flash simultaneously. When you return the lever to its original position, the lights and indicators turn off.

The lever returns to its original position automatically after completing a turn. If the steering wheel angle was small, the lever may not return automatically — return it manually in this case.
## Lane Change Signal
To signal a lane change without latching the lever, lightly press the lever partway in the direction you wish to change and hold it. The turn signal and its indicator will flash while the lever is held. If you release the lever immediately after activating it, the turn signal and indicator will flash 3 times.

> **NOTE:**
> - The number of flashes for the lane change signal (1–4 times) can be customised. Contact your Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
> - Whether the turn signal flashes 3 times after the lever is returned can also be configured via the information display setting mode. Refer to [[12-19 Setting Mode of Information Display (Type B)]] in the "Specifications" section.
