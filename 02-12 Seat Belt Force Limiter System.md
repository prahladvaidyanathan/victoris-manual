# Seat Belt Force Limiter System (Only for Front Seat)

The seat belt force limiter system is designed to operate together with the seat belt pretensioner system. In a frontal impact of sufficient severity, the pretensioner retracts the seat belt, and then the force limiter operates in one stage to reduce the force applied by the shoulder strap on the occupant's chest.

> **WARNING:** If the shoulder anchor or tongue plate become very hot due to heavy friction, the resin may melt and soften. This may cause the belt to malfunction in a collision. If the shoulder anchor or tongue plate shows signs of abnormal heat or damage, have the seat belt replaced at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
