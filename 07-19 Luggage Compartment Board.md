# Luggage Compartment Board
The luggage compartment board divides the cargo area. You can place luggage on top of the board or underneath it. For tall items, the board can be removed entirely from the luggage compartment.

To remove the board, hook your fingers into the designated spaces and pull it out.

The board can also be held open at an angle by engaging the prop-stop mechanism. Do not apply force to the board while it is held open, and do not leave it propped during driving.

> **WARNING:**
> - Do not allow a child to ride in the luggage compartment. The area does not have a structure to accommodate people, and sudden braking or an accident could cause serious injury.
> - Do not carry items that extend higher than the luggage compartment cover, as these can obstruct the driver's rear view, become damaged, or damage the tailgate.

> **CAUTION:** Handle the luggage board carefully when removing or installing, as careless handling may cause injury.

> **NOTICE:** Do not apply undue force to the board when it is propped open, and do not drive with it propped open. Doing so could break the board or the interior.
