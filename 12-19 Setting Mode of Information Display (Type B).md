# Setting Mode of Information Display (Type B)
When the ignition mode is ON and the vehicle is stationary, you can enter into the setting mode of the information display by selecting the setting mode from the menu bar by pressing "<" or ">" of the selector switch (3) to scroll or move the screen.
## How to Switch to the Setting Mode
The steering wheel controls used are:
- **(1)** Up/down selector switch
- **(2)** OK switch
- **(3)** Left/right selector switch

1. To select the setting item that you want to change, select the setting mode from the menu bar and then select the item by moving the up/down selector switch (1).
2. To exit the setting mode, press "<" or ">" of the selector switch (3).

> **NOTE:**
> - The currently selected setting item is surrounded by a frame.
> - If you push and hold the indicator selector knob (1) to enter into the setting mode when the display (C) shows average fuel consumption, average speed, driving time, total idling stop time and total idling fuel saved (if equipped), the value will be reset simultaneously. If you do not want to reset the value, push the indicator selector knob quickly to switch the indication of the display.
> - If you press the engine switch or start to move the vehicle when the display is in the setting mode, the setting mode will be cancelled automatically.
## How to Exit the Setting Mode
Press "<" or ">" of the selector switch (3).

> **NOTE:** You can exit the setting mode by either of the following operations:
> - Press the engine switch to change the ignition mode to ACC or LOCK (OFF).
> - Start driving your vehicle.

## Adjusting the Clock (if equipped)
You can adjust the clock on the information display.

1. When the ignition mode is ON and the vehicle is stationary, push the left/right selector switch (3) to change the information display to the setting mode.
2. Select "Date/Time" and press the "OK" switch (2).
3. Select "Adjust clock" and press the "OK" switch (2).
4. Press selector up/down switch (1) "∧" up or down "∨" to adjust the clock.
5. To change the hour and minute indication press "<" or ">" of the switch (3). Press "OK" switch (2) after adjusting the clock.

> **NOTE:** To exit the setting mode, press "<" or ">" of the selector switch (3).
## Adjusting the Date (if equipped)
You can adjust the date on the information display.

1. When the ignition mode is ON and the vehicle is stationary, push the left/right selector switch (3) to change the information display to the setting mode.
2. Select "Date/Time" and press the "OK" switch (2).
3. Select "Adjust date" and press the "OK" switch (2).
4. Press selector up/down switch (1) "∧" up or down "∨" to adjust the date.
5. To change the day, month and year indication press "<" or ">" of the switch (3). Press "OK" switch (2) after adjusting the date.

> **NOTE:** To exit the setting mode, press "<" or ">" of the selector switch (3).
## Air Conditioning System Settings by ENG A-STOP System (if equipped)
When using the automatic heating and air conditioning system while the engine is stopped automatically by the ENG A-STOP system, you can select one of the following settings via the setting mode of the information display:
- Standard
- Economy
- Comfort

When "Economy" is selected, engine auto stop condition is eased, compared with "Standard". As engine auto stop condition will occur more frequently, the stop time will be longer, leading to reduction of fuel consumption.

When "Comfort" is selected, engine auto stop condition becomes severe, compared with "Standard". As engine auto stop condition will occur less frequently, the stop time will be shorter, leading to more comfortable cabin condition by air conditioner.
