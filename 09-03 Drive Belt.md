# Drive Belt
> [!warning] When the engine is running, keep hands, hair, clothing, tools, etc. away from the moving fan and drive belts.

The drive belt tension is adjusted automatically. If the belt is too loose, any of the following problems can result:

- Insufficient lead-acid battery charging
- Engine overheating
- Poor power steering
- Poor air conditioning
- Excessive belt wear

The belts should also be examined to ensure they are not damaged.

## Service
If the belt needs to be replaced or adjusted, have it done by a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
