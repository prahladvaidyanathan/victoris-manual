# Clutch

| Clutch slipping (dragging or excess damage) | I | I | I | I | I | I | I | I | I | I |

**Clutch fluid:** Replace every 40,000 km or 24 months, whichever comes first.

## Clutch Pedal Play
The clutch pedal play is the distance the pedal moves freely before you feel slight resistance. Measure it by pushing the pedal gently by hand.

**Maximum allowed play: 10 mm**

If the play exceeds 10 mm, or if clutch dragging is felt with the pedal fully depressed, have the clutch inspected at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.

## Clutch Fluid
Clutch fluid is supplied from the brake fluid reservoir. Check the pedal for smooth operation and fluid level periodically.

If the fluid level is near the **MIN** line, top it up to the **MAX** line using **Brake Fluid DOT3**.

> [!notice] If the clutch fluid level is rising or near the MIN line, this may indicate clutch or brake fluid leakage, or abnormal clutch wear. Have the vehicle inspected immediately at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
