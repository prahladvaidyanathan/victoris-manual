# Towing by Using Towing Eye
> **WARNING:** For vehicles equipped with Dual Sensor Brake Support II (DSBS II), if your vehicle is towed with the engine on, press the DSBS II OFF switch and turn off the system. If not, an accident related to the system being turned on may occur.

## Hanging Point of a Towing Cable
Hang a towing cable on a towing eye. The towing eye is provided on the front of the vehicle for use in emergency situations or sea shipping purposes.

## Installing the Towing Eye
1. Remove the cover by using a jack handle covered with a soft cloth, as shown in the illustration.

> **NOTICE:** Do not apply excessive force to the cover strap, as it may be damaged. It is recommended to fasten the cover with tape to prevent damage during towing.

2. Install the towing eye by hand.
3. To tighten the towing eye, turn it clockwise using a wheel wrench until it is securely installed.

> **NOTICE:** Observe the following when using the towing eye, otherwise the towing eye or the vehicle body may break and cause serious injury or damage:
> - Avoid pulling the vehicle to get out of severe snowy, muddy or sandy conditions.
> - Avoid sudden starts or erratic driver behaviour which could cause excessive stress on the towing eye.
> - Do not use the towing eye for towing another vehicle or transporting your vehicle by a car carrier trailer.
>
> In such cases, contact a professional service.

## Rear Frame Hook (K15C Engine)
The frame hook on the rear of the vehicle is provided for emergency situations and sea shipping purposes only. Do not use the frame hook for towing another vehicle, being towed by another vehicle, or transporting your vehicle by a car carrier trailer. Otherwise, the frame hook or the vehicle body may break and cause serious injury or damage.

## Car Carrier Frame Holes
The frame holes on the underside of the vehicle are provided only for transporting by a car carrier trailer. Do not use these holes for towing — they may break and cause serious injury or damage.

## Towing by Using a Rope
1. Hang a towing cable on the towing eye horizontally, so that the hanging side is at the same height for both the towing vehicle and the towed vehicle if possible.
2. Keep the engine running.
   - If you cannot start the engine, press the engine switch or power switch to change the ignition mode to a position other than LOCK (OFF).
   - Turn the steering wheel left and right to confirm the steering lock is released.
   - If the vehicle has Suzuki Safety Support, when the ignition is in ON mode, press the DSBS II OFF switch and turn off the system.
3. Shift the gearshift lever into **N**.

> **NOTE:** If you cannot shift the gearshift lever into N, towing by rope is not possible. Consult a Maruti Suzuki authorized workshop.

4. During towing, make sure the towing cable is not slack. Carefully drive your vehicle watching the brake lights of the front vehicle. Turn on the hazard warning lights of the towed vehicle to warn following drivers.

> **WARNING:** When your vehicle is towed by rope, observe the following:
> - Do not press the engine switch or power switch to change the ignition mode to LOCK (OFF) — the steering wheel will lock and cannot be turned.
> - Depress the brake pedal more firmly than usual, as the brake booster does not work.
> - Operate the steering wheel with more force, as the power steering does not work.
> - If you cannot release the steering lock due to a malfunction and a flat lead-acid battery, do not perform towing by rope.
> - If you continuously depress the brake pedal on a long or steep downhill slope, the brakes could overheat and become ineffective. Do not tow by rope on such roads — request a tow truck instead.

> **NOTICE:** Observe the following to avoid damage:
> - Do not apply a strong impact to a towing eye or cable by sudden starting. Do not accelerate suddenly.
> - Tow on roads only. Do not exceed 30 km/h and do not tow for longer than 30 km, to protect the transmission.
> - Tow the vehicle in the forward direction only.
