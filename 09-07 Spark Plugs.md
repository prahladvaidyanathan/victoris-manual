# Spark Plugs
## Maintenance Schedule — K15C Engine
| Fuel type | Replacement interval |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Petrol | Every 100,000 km or 84 months, whichever comes first |
| CNG | Every 50,000 km or 48 months, whichever comes first |

## Service
Since special procedures, materials, and tools are required, spark plug inspection and replacement should be done at a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
