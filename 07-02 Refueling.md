# Refueling
> **WARNING:** Observe the following points when refueling to prevent fire:
> - Always stop the engine first.
> - Close all doors and windows when refueling.
> - As gasoline is highly flammable, open sparks such as lit cigarettes are strictly prohibited.
> - If you open the fuel filler cap suddenly, fuel may be under pressure and spray out. Loosen the cap slowly and wait for the air-deflating sound to stop before opening fully.
> - Insert the fuel filler nozzle securely into the fuel filler opening until it stops.
> - Pull the gas pump filler nozzle lever until it stops.
> - When the automatic stop function of the gas pump nozzle activates, stop refueling. Do not continue to fill beyond this point.
> - If fuel spills out, wipe it off immediately with a soft cloth. Spilled fuel can cause paint staining, discoloration, or cracking, and is a fire hazard.
> - After refueling, close the fuel filler cap until you hear more than 2 clicks.
> - Fuel contains substances harmful to the human body. Do not breathe in vaporized fuel.
> - Follow all warnings posted at the gas station.
## At a Self-Service Gas Station
> **WARNING:** Before opening the fuel filler cap, discharge any static electricity by touching a metal part of the vehicle body or the gas pump machine. Do not re-enter the vehicle while refueling, as static electricity may re-accumulate in your body. Do not allow others who have not discharged their static electricity to come near the fuel filler opening.

> **NOTE:** Take special care when the air is dry, as this increases the likelihood of static electricity buildup. Wearing multiple layers of clothing can also generate static electricity through fiber friction.
## Use Unleaded Gasoline Only
> **NOTICE:** Using leaded gasoline, low-quality gasoline, or other fuel types such as alcohol or diesel can cause harmful effects to the engine and fuel delivery system. Use only unleaded gasoline.
