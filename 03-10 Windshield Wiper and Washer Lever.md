# Windshield Wiper and Washer Lever
The wiper/washer lever can be used when the ignition mode is ON.

> **CAUTION:** In cold weather, sprayed washer fluid on the glass may freeze and cause poor visibility. Use the defroster to warm the glass before spraying washer fluid. Refer to [[07-21 Heating and Air Conditioning System]] in the "Other Controls and Equipment" section.

> **NOTICE:**
> - Do not use the wipers on a dry windshield. The glass and wiper blade rubbers may be damaged. Always use washer fluid first.
> - Do not operate the wipers if the wiper blades are stuck to the glass, as the rubbers may be damaged or the wipers broken.
> - Do not continue holding the washer lever when no fluid is being sprayed, as this can damage the washer pump.
> - Clear ice or packed snow from the wiper blades before using the wipers.
> - Check the washer fluid level regularly, especially in bad weather.
## Windshield Wipers
Move the wiper lever (1) upward or downward to operate the wipers.

| Position | Function |
|---|---|
| MIST | Temporary operation only while the lever is held up |
| OFF | Off |
| INT | Intermittent operation |
| LO | Low speed operation |
| HI | High speed operation |

To adjust the intermittent wiper interval, turn the "INT TIME" control on the lever forward or backward to the desired setting.
## Windshield Washer
Pull the lever (1) toward you to spray windshield washer fluid. The windshield wipers will automatically operate a few times.

> **WARNING:**
> - To prevent windshield icing in cold weather, turn on the defroster to heat the windshield before and during washer use.
> - Do not use radiator antifreeze in the windshield washer reservoir. It can severely impair visibility when sprayed on the windshield and can damage your vehicle's paint.
## When the Wipers Suddenly Stop
If the wipers are under heavy load (e.g. covered by snow) and the wiper motor temperature becomes too high, a breaker will activate and the wipers will temporarily stop to protect the motor from overheating.

If this happens:
1. Stop the vehicle in a safe place and change the ignition mode to ACC or LOCK (OFF).
2. Move the wiper lever to the "OFF" position.
3. Remove any obstacles such as snow from the wipers.
4. After a while, once the motor temperature drops, the breaker will reset automatically and the wipers can be used again.

If the wipers cannot be used after a while, there may be another problem. Have the wipers inspected by a Maruti Suzuki authorised workshop.
## Rear Window Wiper
Twist the lever (2) to operate the rear window wiper.

| Position | Function |
|---|---|
| ON | Normal operation |
| INT | Intermittent operation |
| OFF | Off |

> **NOTICE:** Clear ice or snow from the rear window and rear wiper blade before using the rear wiper. Accumulated ice or snow could prevent the blade from moving and damage the wiper motor.
## Rear Window Washer
Twist the lever (2) past the "ON" or "OFF" position to the washer symbol to spray the rear window washer fluid. The rear wiper will operate simultaneously. When you release the lever, it returns to the "ON" or "OFF" position respectively.
