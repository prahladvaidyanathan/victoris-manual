# Audio Function
Keep audio volume at a level that allows you to remain aware of your surroundings while driving.

> **NOTE:**
> - If a mobile phone is used inside or near the vehicle while audio is playing, noise may be heard from the speakers. This is not a malfunction.
> - Navigation and audio equipment may not function properly if interfered with by unauthorised radio frequency transmitters.

For operating the audio or navigation system, refer to the infotainment system manual provided with the vehicle.
