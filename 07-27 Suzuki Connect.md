# Suzuki Connect
Suzuki Connect is an advanced telematics solution that provides connected car features including vehicle alerts and notifications, safety and security functions, trip tracking, and remote vehicle operation.

For full details on feature availability, terms and conditions, FAQs, the Suzuki Connect app manual, and contact information, refer to the Maruti Suzuki website.
## Remote AC
The Remote AC feature allows you to set the cabin temperature remotely (18–32 °C) for up to 10 minutes to pre-condition the car before you board. You can also turn the defroster and rear defogger on or off remotely. The remote request is authenticated through your preset mPIN after your consent is given.

The feature can be operated from the Suzuki Connect app, a compatible smartwatch, or the Suzuki Connect skill for Amazon Alexa.
### Operating Conditions
The Remote AC request will only be processed when **all** of the following conditions are met:
- Ignition is OFF
- Parking brake is engaged
- All doors are locked properly
- Hood is closed properly
- Brake pedal is not depressed
- Gearshift lever is in "P" (Park) position
- Key fob is not detected inside the vehicle
- Steering lock is engaged
- Vehicle is in a good cellular network area
- Less than 9 days have passed since the last ignition OFF
- No malfunction was present in the vehicle before ignition OFF
### Terminating Conditions
The Remote AC session will end if any of the following occur:
- Telematics device loses communication with the vehicle
- Battery voltage is low
- Any door, hood, or tailgate is opened
- Key fob is detected inside the vehicle
- Vehicle moves or ignition is turned ON
- Brake pedal is applied
- Gearshift lever is moved out of "P"
- AC malfunction is detected
- Engine stalls

> **WARNING:**
> - Accumulation of exhaust gases in an enclosed area may cause serious health issues or death. Ensure there are no passengers in the vehicle before using this feature. Park only in areas where it is safe to use, and avoid enclosed spaces such as garages or multi-level car parks. Ensure ventilation openings and the exhaust muffler are not blocked. Do not use if flammable material such as dry leaves or oil is near the vehicle, and do not use if the vehicle is covered with a body cover.
> - Never use this feature when there are passengers in the vehicle. The sudden engine start, change in cabin temperature, and unexpected operation may cause anxiety, panic, or discomfort.
> - Never use this feature while the vehicle is under inspection or maintenance at a service centre or fuel station. A sudden engine start during maintenance is a safety hazard.

> **CAUTION:**
> - The following may activate as a side effect of Remote AC: windscreen wipers (if left in ON position), audio system.
> - The desired temperature may not be achieved if any window is open.

> **NOTICE:**
> - Do not use this feature on public roads.
> - Depending on local regulations, idling may be prohibited or restricted. Check before use.
> - Use the feature only when you are ready to board promptly, for effective fuel efficiency.

> **NOTE:**
> - Application services are subject to change. Install the latest updates from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store for the best experience.
> - Application functioning depends on network availability and smartphone hardware and OS performance.
